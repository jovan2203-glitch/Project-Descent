# Patch notes — 23 October 2026 (0.0.22)

The Frost talent tree moves to the column-group layout: its five specs sit side by side, each with 19 talents and a capstone, and most talents now work with the book's keywords instead of a single ability. Every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load. Damage is given at level 50 before gear.

## Fire

- Changed: the talent Light String is renamed Easy Draw.

## Frost

### Talents

The tree has 11 columns: Hard Winter on the left, then Cold Comfort, the open talents in the middle, Frostbitten Steel, Glacier Wall and Winter Hunt on the right. Rows 1 to 9 hold two talents per spec. Row 10 holds each spec's 5-rank capstone, which needs 25 points spent in that spec. The tree no longer has links.

Hard Winter:
- New talent: Glass Cold (row 1, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells deal 3 / 6 / 9% more damage to targets you have Frozen.
- New talent: Ice Needles (row 5, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells that critically strike have a 33 / 66 / 100% chance to give you an Ice Shard charge.
- New talent: Snowdrift (row 7, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells deal 4 / 8 / 12% more damage to a target with 2 or more other enemies within 8 m of it.
- New talent: Cold Front (row 7, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells deal 1 / 2 / 3% more damage for each enemy you have Chilled or Frozen, up to 5.
- New talent: Cold Well (row 9, 3 ranks). Freezing an enemy gives you 20 / 41 / 61 mana, at most once every 1 sec.
- Changed: Frozen Instinct now makes your next Frost spell with a cast time instant and free (was your next Frost Bolt). It no longer requires Frost Bolt.
- Changed: Splintering Cold no longer requires Ice Shards.
- Changed: Splintering now has 2 ranks (was 1) and splinters into 1 / 2 random other enemies (was 1), each for 46 Frost damage.
- Moved: Bitter Cold, Quickened Frost, Deep Hoarfrost, Brittle Rime, Shardstorm, Piercing Cold, Cold Snap, Lingering Frost, Hard Freeze and Shatterpoint to Hard Winter's columns. Deep Freeze is its capstone.

Cold Comfort:
- New talent: Thaw Water (row 2, 3 ranks). Your heals on an ally with your Thawing heal 4 / 8 / 12% more.
- New talent: Last Shelter (row 3, 3 ranks). Your Frost shields (Rime Shell, Rime Veil, Rimefall, Shard Spray) absorb 5 / 10 / 15% more on an ally below 50% health.
- New talent: Cold Hands (row 3, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells that heal or shield cost 3 / 6 / 9% less mana.
- New talent: Meltdown (row 4, 3 ranks). When your Rime Shell breaks, you gain 20 / 41 / 61 mana, at most once every 2 sec.
- New talent: Long Frost (row 5, 3 ranks). Your Rime Veil, Rimefall and Shard Spray shields last 2 / 4 / 6 sec longer.
- New talent: Shard Reach (row 5, 2 ranks). Your Rime Shards reach allies 2 / 4 m farther away.
- New talent: Snowmelt (row 6, 3 ranks). Your Runoff heals 15 / 30 / 45% faster, for the same total.
- New talent: Layered Veil (row 7, 2 ranks, requires Rime Veil). Rime Veil forms again 0.5 / 1 sec sooner.
- New talent: Ice Bath (row 7, 3 ranks). Your heals on an ally carrying one of your Frost shields heal 3 / 6 / 9% more.
- New talent: Deep Snow (row 8, 3 ranks, requires Rimefall). Rimefall's shields absorb 10 / 20 / 30% more.
- New talent: Cold Rush (row 8, 3 ranks). Your Frost spells that heal or shield cast 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 sec faster on an ally below 50% health.
- New talent: Close Weave (row 9, 3 ranks, requires Frost Lattice). Your heals on an ally linked by your Frost Lattice heal 5 / 10 / 15% more.
- New talent: Frozen Hearth (row 10, 5 ranks, capstone). Your Frost shields absorb 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10% more, and your Rime Shards heal 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10% more.
- Changed: Deep Cracks, Shard Spray and Slow Runoff no longer require Rime Shell.
- Changed: Wide Lattice now has 2 ranks (was 1) and links 1 / 2 more allies (was 1).
- Moved: Cold Spread and Splinterheal to Cold Comfort's columns.

Open talents:
- New talent: Winter Coat (row 9, 3 ranks). You take 1 / 2 / 3% less damage from spells.
- Moved: Frozen Focus, Frozen Mind, Rime Ward, Hardened Frost, Ice Floe, Numbing Cold, Glacial Heart and Cold Discipline to the open column in the middle of the tree (rows 1 to 8).

Frostbitten Steel:
- New talent: Sharp Glaze (row 1, 3 ranks). Your Frost abilities deal 1 / 2 / 3% more damage for each Glaze you hold.
- New talent: Glaze Hunger (row 2, 3 ranks). When you gain Glaze, you have a 10 / 20 / 30% chance to gain one more.
- New talent: Hard Glaze (row 3, 3 ranks). Each Glaze your finishers (Calving, Icebreaker) spend gives you 2 / 4 / 6 Fury.
- New talent: Splitting Cold (row 5, 3 ranks). Your finishers deal 4 / 8 / 12% more damage to a target you have Frozen.
- New talent: Shiver (row 6, 3 ranks). Your critical strikes on a target you have Frozen have a 33 / 66 / 100% chance to give you a Glaze, at most once every 2 sec.
- New talent: Rime Grip (row 7, 3 ranks). Your auto-attacks on a target you have Chilled give you 1 / 2 / 3 more Fury.
- New talent: Cold Swing (row 7, 3 ranks). Your melee abilities that hit 2 or more enemies give you 3 / 6 / 9 Fury.
- New talent: Thaw Strike (row 8, 3 ranks). When a target you have Frozen thaws, your next finisher within 10 sec costs 5 / 10 / 15 less Fury.
- New talent: Quick Rime (row 9, 3 ranks). Each Glaze you hold gives you 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.9% attack speed.
- New talent: Frost Heave (row 9, 3 ranks). A finisher that spends 5 Glaze has a 33 / 66 / 100% chance to Freeze its target.
- Changed: Third Bite now works on any Frost ability that Freezes its target, for 20 / 40 / 60% more damage (was the Winter's Bite that Freezes, for 27 / 53 / 80%). It no longer requires Winter's Bite.
- Changed: Clean Break now works for both finishers (was Icebreaker only): 7 / 13 / 20 Fury back when they hit a target you have Frozen. It no longer requires Icebreaker.
- Moved: Long Glaze, Cold Sweep, Honed Rime, Ice Fall, Biting Hold and Long Dark to Frostbitten Steel's columns. Glinting Rime is its capstone.

Glacier Wall:
- New talent: Lasting Ice (row 2, 3 ranks). Your Ice Layers last 3 / 6 / 9 sec longer.
- New talent: Deep Ice (row 2, 2 ranks). You can hold 1 / 2 more Ice Layers.
- New talent: Spent Ice (row 3, 3 ranks). Each Ice Layer Shattering Slam shatters into a target gives you 2 / 4 / 6 Fury.
- New talent: Hoar Coat (row 4, 3 ranks). Each Ice Layer you gain heals you for 0.3 / 0.6 / 1% of your maximum health, with its own healing-meter line.
- New talent: Cold Retort (row 4, 3 ranks). Each of your Ice Layers that breaks deals 23 / 46 / 69 Frost damage to the attacker, with its own damage-meter line.
- New talent: Rime Pressure (row 5, 3 ranks). Your spells and abilities generate 1 / 2 / 3% more threat for each Ice Layer you hold.
- New talent: Reforming (row 6, 3 ranks). Each of your Ice Layers that breaks has a 5 / 10 / 15% chance to form again at once.
- New talent: Cracking Ice (row 7, 3 ranks). Each of your Ice Layers that breaks takes 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 sec off the cooldowns of your Frost abilities.
- New talent: Steady Footing (row 7, 3 ranks). While you hold 3 or more Ice Layers, you take 2 / 4 / 6% less damage.
- New talent: Rime Armor (row 8, 3 ranks). Each Ice Layer you hold increases your Armor by 1 / 2 / 3%.
- New talent: Full Strata (row 9, 3 ranks). While you hold all the Ice Layers you can, your abilities deal 5 / 10 / 15% more damage.
- Moved: Packed Ice, Cold Fury, Deep Tremor, Turned Backs, Regelation, Old Ice and Chilling Presence to Glacier Wall's columns. Deep Strata is its capstone.

Winter Hunt:
- New talent: Frost Fletching (row 3, 3 ranks). Your auto-shots deal 3 / 6 / 9% more damage to targets you have Chilled.
- New talent: Rime Tips (row 4, 3 ranks). Your auto-shots have a 5 / 10 / 15% chance to Chill their target.
- New talent: Marked Ice (row 5, 3 ranks). Your arrow abilities that hit a target you have Chilled have a 10 / 20 / 30% chance to give you an Ice Shard charge.
- New talent: Winter Quiver (row 6, 3 ranks). Your arrow abilities that critically strike give you 4 / 8 / 12 Energy.
- New talent: Splitting Arrow (row 6, 3 ranks). Your arrows that hit a target you have Frozen splinter into one other enemy within 8 m, dealing 10 / 20 / 30% of their damage as Frost damage, with its own damage-meter line.
- New talent: Deep Pin (row 7, 3 ranks). Your roots last 1 / 2 / 3 sec longer.
- New talent: Numb Prey (row 7, 3 ranks). Your auto-shots deal 3 / 6 / 9% more damage to targets you have Frozen.
- New talent: Swift Ice (row 8, 3 ranks). Your auto-shots and auto-attacks are 2 / 4 / 6% faster against a target you have Chilled.
- New talent: Ice Hunger (row 8, 3 ranks). Freezing an enemy gives you 5 / 10 / 15 Energy.
- New talent: Cold Eye (row 9, 3 ranks). Your arrows that critically strike a target you have Frozen deal 5 / 10 / 15% more damage.
- New talent: Sleet Arrows (row 9, 2 ranks). Your arrow abilities that Chill their target also Chill the 1 / 2 enemies nearest it, within 8 m.
- New talent: Bitter Hunt (row 10, 5 ranks, capstone). Your arrows deal 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5% more damage for each of your slows on the target (Chilled, Frozen and rooted each count).
- Changed: Marked Prey is now Frozen Quarry: your arrows deal 4 / 8 / 12% more damage to targets you have Frozen (was your Ice Shards dealing 10 / 20 / 30% more to your Coldmark target). It no longer requires Coldmark.
- Changed: Pinning Frost now works for all your arrows against any target you have rooted, for 5 / 10 / 15% more damage (was Frost Arrow against a target rooted by your Icepin, for 13 / 27 / 40%). It no longer requires Icepin.
- Moved: Cold Draw, Black Ice, Clean Shatter, Cold Harvest and Thin Ice to Winter Hunt's columns.

## Lightning

- Changed: the talent Live Current is renamed Quick Pulse, so it no longer shares the caster spec's name.
- Changed: the talent Lightning Rod is renamed Grounded Arrow, so it no longer shares the tank spec's name.

## Arcane

- Changed: the talent Clockwork is renamed Spare Seconds, so it no longer shares the caster spec's name.

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load, because the Frost tree changed.
