# Patch notes — 8 October 2026

## Arcane tank
A new way to play the Arcane book: a dodge tank that fights with a melee weapon in each hand. It has no resource bar; every ability runs on its cooldown alone. All eight abilities require a one-handed melee weapon in the Main Hand and another in the Off Hand.

### Reflections
- A Reflection is an image of your character. They stand to your left, right, front and back, and you can have up to 4.
- Each Reflection gives 5% Dodge and raises your Dodge cap by 5%: the cap is 30% with none and 50% with four.
- When you dodge an attack, one Reflection takes it and shatters. A shatter deals Arcane damage (138 at level 50 before gear) with 4x threat to enemies within 5 m, and takes 0.5 sec off the cooldown of every Arcane spell and ability you have on cooldown. Interrupts are not affected.
- Reflections last 30 sec. Gaining one makes all of them last 30 sec again.
- Shatter damage has its own line on the damage meter: Reflection Shatter.
- "Reflection" is a keyword: it is shown in orange and explained when you hold Shift.
- Your party sees your Reflections around your character.

### New abilities
- New ability: Double Up (Skill Card level 4, 5 sec cooldown). Strikes the target with both weapons, dealing 100% of the Main Hand and Off Hand weapons' combined damage as Arcane damage with 4x threat, and creates a Reflection.
- New ability: Ripple (Skill Card level 10, 8 sec cooldown). Deals Arcane damage (230 at level 50 before gear) with 4x threat to all enemies within 6 m. Creates a Reflection if it hits 3 or more enemies. Up to 5 enemies take full damage; with more, that damage is split evenly between all of them.
- New ability: Missed Beat (Skill Card level 12, 3 sec cooldown, off the global cooldown). Usable within 4 sec of one of your Reflections shattering. Strikes the target with both weapons, dealing 100% of the Main Hand and Off Hand weapons' combined damage as Arcane damage with 4x threat.
- New ability: Full Set (Skill Card level 16, 30 sec cooldown). Creates Reflections until you have 4.
- New ability: Stand-In (Skill Card level 22, 25 sec cooldown). For 4 sec, each attack that gets through to you destroys one of your Reflections instead of hitting you: you take no damage and none of its effects, and that Reflection shatters. Damage over time is not stopped.
- New ability: Trade Places (Skill Card level 28, 30 sec cooldown, off the global cooldown). Gives up all your Reflections. For each one, heals you for 20% of the damage you took in the last 3 sec. Reflections given up this way do not shatter.
- New ability: Decoy (Skill Card level 32, 90 sec cooldown). Sends one of your Reflections to a spot up to 20 m away. Enemies within 8 m of it that can see it are forced to attack it for 3 sec. Then it bursts, dealing Arcane damage (230 at level 50 before gear) with 4x threat to all enemies within 5 m. The Decoy cannot be killed; attacks on it do nothing.
- New ability: Crowd of One (Skill Card level 38, 90 sec cooldown). For 8 sec, a Reflection that takes an attack you dodge still shatters, but is not used up.

### New talents
The tank's talents run down both edges of the Arcane tree. Rows 2, 3 and 9 each gained a slot on both edges; the caster's talents did not move.

Left edge, making and shattering Reflections:
- New talent: Sharp Edges (row 2, 3 ranks). Your Reflections deal 10/20/30% more damage when they shatter. Capstone: a shatter reaches enemies within 7 m of you instead of 5 m.
- New talent: Clockwork (row 3, 2 ranks). Each Reflection that shatters takes 0.25/0.5 sec more off the cooldowns of your Arcane spells and abilities, for 0.75/1 sec in total.
- New talent: Spare Copy (row 4, 2 ranks). When you dodge an attack, there is a 10/20% chance that the Reflection which takes it is not used up. It still shatters.
- New talent: Second Self (row 5, 2 ranks, requires Double Up). Double Up has a 15/30% chance to create 2 Reflections.
- New talent: Wide Rings (row 7, 2 ranks, requires Ripple). Ripple reaches 1/2 m further. Capstone: it creates a Reflection when it hits 2 or more enemies instead of 3.
- New talent: On the Beat (row 8, 3 ranks, requires Missed Beat). Missed Beat deals 10/20/30% more damage. Capstone: used while you have no Reflections, it creates one.
- New talent: Head Count (row 9, 2 ranks, requires Full Set). Full Set heals you for 2/4% of your maximum health for each Reflection it creates. This healing has its own line on the meter: Head Count.

Right edge, staying alive:
- New talent: Light Feet (row 2, 3 ranks). Increases your Dodge chance by 1/2/3% while you wield a melee weapon in each hand.
- New talent: Empty Room (row 3, 3 ranks). While you wield a melee weapon in each hand and have no Reflections, you take 4/8/12% less damage.
- New talent: Bent Light (row 4, 2 ranks). You take 1/2% less non-Physical damage for each Reflection you have, up to 4/8% with four.
- New talent: Understudy (row 5, 2 ranks, requires Stand-In). Each Reflection destroyed by Stand-In takes 2/4 sec off its cooldown.
- New talent: Loud Exit (row 7, 1 rank, requires Trade Places). The Reflections you give up with Trade Places shatter: each one deals its shatter damage, takes its time off your cooldowns and lets Missed Beat be used.
- New talent: Good Likeness (row 8, 2 ranks, requires Decoy). Decoy holds enemies for 1/2 sec longer. Capstone: Decoy no longer needs or uses up a Reflection.

Links:
- Maxing Sharp Edges unlocks Clockwork, and maxing Clockwork unlocks Spare Copy.
- Maxing Light Feet unlocks Empty Room, and maxing Empty Room unlocks Bent Light.

Tooltips:
- Ripple's tooltip shows its radius with Wide Rings and the number of enemies it needs with the capstone.
- Decoy's tooltip shows how long it holds enemies with Good Likeness.
- Missed Beat's tooltip shows its damage with On the Beat.

### New rune
- New rune: Rune of the Two-Way Mirror (Rare, level 26, one per character). Mirror Sigil also repeats the damage of your Reflections when they shatter, at the same share it repeats your Arcane spells at: 35%, 38/41/45% with Polished Glass, 20% with the Rune of the Carried Sigil.

### Action bar
- Full Set, Trade Places and Decoy show how many Reflections you have on their button.
- Trade Places and Decoy are dimmed while you have no Reflections. Missed Beat is dimmed until a Reflection shatters.

## Dual wield
- One-handed melee weapons (swords and daggers) can be equipped in the Off Hand.
- Equipping a one-handed melee weapon while your Main Hand already holds one and your Off Hand is free puts it in the Off Hand. It can also be dragged onto either slot.
- The Off Hand weapon adds its stats. Double Up and Missed Beat strike with both weapons as a single hit. All other abilities and auto attacks use the Main Hand weapon's damage.
- The detailed tooltip (Shift) of Double Up and Missed Beat lists the Off Hand weapon's damage on its own line.
- A two-handed weapon still takes both hands.
- Every character receives a Steel Dagger in the bank, once.

## Stats and combat text
- The Dodge row of the character sheet shows your Dodge up to your current cap, which is higher while you have Reflections.
- An attack taken by a Reflection during Stand-In shows "Reflection" over your character.
