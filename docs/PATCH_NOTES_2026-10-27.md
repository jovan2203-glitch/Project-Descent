# Patch notes — 27 October 2026

The Shadow, Nature and Holy talent trees are rebuilt on ribbons, like Martial, Fire, Frost, Lightning and Arcane: every book now uses the ribbon layout. Every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load.

## Shadow

### Talents

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load, because the Shadow, Nature and Holy trees changed.
- Changed: clicking a talent you can't put a point in, or trying to take back a point other talents need, now makes the talents involved glow in the Nature and Holy books too, as in every other book.
- Changed: the launcher now patches an installed game instead of downloading all of it again. Only the files that changed are downloaded (about 5 MB for a patch like 0.0.23, instead of 137 MB), and the download size shows before you update. A fresh install, or an update that fails partway, still downloads the full game. This needs the new launcher and starts with the next update after this one.
