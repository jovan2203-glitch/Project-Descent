# Patch notes — 21 October 2026 (0.0.18)

The first step of the talent rework: a talent that only improves one ability should change how that ability plays, and a single spec should take most of your talent points to complete. New talents built around resources, keywords and damage types come in a later patch.

Most two-rank talents that need one ability now have three ranks, with the same value at max rank, so each point is worth a little less and a full spec costs more points. Talents whose steps are whole numbers (extra targets, darts, Reflections, Cracks, Flow, arrows, Incense) keep two ranks. Thirteen talents that only shortened a cooldown, a cast or a cost are now part of their ability at full value; their slots are empty for now, and the points spent in them are refunded.
