# Patch notes — 26 October 2026 (0.0.23)

The Martial, Fire, Frost, Lightning and Arcane talent trees get a look of their own. Each spec is now a bookmark ribbon hanging down the page, its talents are wax seals that together form the spec's own symbol, and the open talents are beads on a thread down the book's spine. Many talents move up or down a row to make the shapes, and new links let a maxed talent open a deeper one early. Every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load.

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: the Martial, Fire, Frost, Lightning and Arcane talent trees are drawn as bookmark ribbons, one per spec and three seals wide. A spec's name and its points toward its capstone (out of 25) sit on a clasp at the top of its ribbon.
- Changed: a ribbon unrolls as you go deeper. It reaches one row past the deepest talent you have points in, and a faint stitched outline shows the rest.
- Changed: talents are wax seals in the book's colour. Unspent ones are empty stamp outlines, and talents you can't spend points in yet are faint. Each seal shows its rank as a bold number (for example 2/3) on a tab at its bottom edge, gold once maxed, and a seal squashes slightly when you spend a point. Seal icons sit on a dark backing so they stand out from the wax; talents with no points in them stay grayed out.
- Changed: each spec's capstone hangs at the tail of its ribbon, with a gold ring that fills as you approach 25 points in that spec.
- Changed: the open talents are beads on a stitched thread down the book's spine, between the ribbons.
- Changed: links are drawn as stitched thread between seals and turn gold once the first talent is maxed.
- Changed: the in-game Spellbook shows the same ribbons and is a little wider to fit them.
- Changed: every talent point in every book is refunded on your next load, because the trees changed.
- Changed: every ability now has a little more flair, in its book's colour. While you cast, your hand glows, motes gather into it and a rune circle turns at your feet. When an ability goes off, there is a flash at your hand and a pulse along the ground (martial abilities throw sparks instead). Melee swings throw sparks, and your heals send motes rising around whoever you healed. Party members see your cast, release and swing effects too.
- Changed: you can only party with players on the same version of the game. Joining a party on another version is refused with a message naming both versions, and the group finder only matches parties on your version. Your version is shown under the Play button.
- Changed: an "Open Logs Folder" button in the menu opens the folder with the game's log files, to send with bug reports. Each log now records which version of the game wrote it.
- Changed: in a party, the FPS / Stats overlay (F3) shows your ping (to the host, or to each member if you are the host), how much the game sends per second and the party size.
- Changed: the in-game Spellbook is larger and easier to read: a taller window, bigger ability buttons, wider talent ribbons and larger text.
- Changed: in the training area, talents can be changed from the in-game Spellbook. Left click spends a point, right click takes one back, and a choice talent offers its two options to pick from. Elsewhere in the game the Spellbook's talents stay view only.
- Changed: you can inspect a party member. Click their card in the lobby, or right-click their party frame in game. The Inspect window shows their gear (hover an item for its stats and runes), their stats, the runes on their gear, the abilities on their action bars (tooltips use their stats) and their talents. Esc or the X closes it.
- Changed: clicking a talent you can't put points in yet makes the talents that would open it pulse with light (the row above that still needs points, its linked talent, or its spec for a capstone). Trying to take back a point that other talents depend on makes those talents pulse red. This works in the main menu and in the training area's Spellbook.
- Changed: error messages now appear in a dark box with a coloured frame, low on the screen. In the main menu they sit just above the XP bar; in game, above the cast bar (still movable with Move UI as "Messages").
- New: the chat command /testkit <spec> (also /testkit_<spec>, e.g. /testkit venomstring) makes you ready to play that spec. You are set to level 50 and learn the spec's abilities and its book's Utility, all placed on your bars (extra bars switch on when needed). You get a full set of Epic item-level-50 gear with the spec's stats, the right armor type and weapons all its abilities can use, and your talent points are spent for the spec. No runes. Your old gear goes to the bank, and talents and bars can be changed as usual afterwards.
- New: the chat command /bug <what happened> notes a bug in your log with your name, where you are and the party size, so it can be found after a session.
- Changed: the log writes a performance line every 30 seconds: FPS, enemy count, ping and network traffic.
