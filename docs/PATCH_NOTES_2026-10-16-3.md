# Patch notes — 16 October 2026 (third update)

Heals and damage are given at level 50 before gear. Mana costs are in mana points; one point is 10% of your base mana. Bow abilities are given with a basic bow.

## Nature

The Nature book is rebuilt around creatures woven out of vines or grown out of the ground. Each one appears for a single strike, heal or block, then unravels into leaves or sinks back into the earth. There are no pets. Your party sees your creatures faintly.

- Removed: Wrath, Insect Swarm, Entangling Roots and Regrowth. Their Skill Cards are gone.

### Nature caster: the Weaver (new playstyle: Thornweaver)
- Mulch: your woven creatures (Briar Tiger, Thornwing) leave a patch of mulch where they strike, and every 3rd Thornshot that lands leaves one too. You can have 5 patches; each lasts 20 sec, and a 6th replaces the oldest. Your rooted creatures (Mossback, Choking Vipers) use up every patch within 8 m of where they strike and grow stronger with each. Only your own creatures use your mulch; your party sees it faint.
- A Mulch buff shows how many patches you have, and Mossback and Choking Vipers show the count on their buttons. Their tooltips show the damage with your current patches and with 5.
- Mossback and Choking Vipers can be cast at the enemy under your mouse (with Mouseover casting on). Otherwise an 8 m circle appears: click to place it on the patches you want to use. Pressing the ability again puts the circle away.
- New ability: Thornshot (Skill Card level 2, 1.5 sec cast, 1 mana point, 30 m range). Deals 460 Nature damage. When it lands, thorns burst from every patch of your mulch within 8 m of the target, dealing 184 Nature damage to each enemy within 4 m of a patch (each enemy once per cast; its own line on the damage meter: Thornburst).
- New ability: Briar Tiger (Skill Card level 4, 1.5 sec cast, 6 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 30 m range). A tiger woven of vines pounces on the target for 690 Nature damage and leaves a patch of mulch.
- New ability: Mossback (Skill Card level 6, instant, 2 mana points, 30 m range). A bear grows out of your mulch and mauls the target for 276 Nature damage, plus 276 for each patch it uses (1,656 with 5).
- New ability: Thornwing (Skill Card level 8, instant, 10 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 30 m range). A hawk dives on the target from above for 460 Nature damage (it ignores line of sight and can be cast while moving) and leaves a patch of mulch.
- New ability: Choking Vipers (Skill Card level 10, instant, 3 mana points, 30 m range). Vipers rise from your mulch and coil around every enemy within 8 m, dealing 184 Nature damage over 3 sec, plus 184 for each patch used (1,104 with 5), and holding each in place for 0.5 sec. They can still attack and cast. Full damage to up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them.
- New ability: Greenwood Stag (Skill Card level 12, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 30 m range). A stag grows from your mulch and bounds to the target for 230 Nature damage, carrying every patch of yours with it and setting them down in a ring around the target.
- New talent: Hunting Party (row 1, 4 ranks, requires Briar Tiger). Briar Tiger deals 5 / 10 / 15% more damage. At full rank, every second Briar Tiger also strikes 2 more enemies within 8 m of the target and leaves a patch of mulch under each.

### Nature healer: the Shepherd (new playstyle: Shepherd)
- Blossom: your woven creatures (Fawn, Owl) leave a Blossom on each ally they heal, or two when the heal is more than the ally was missing. An ally holds up to 3; they last 20 sec, and a new one starts the time of all of them over. Your rooted creatures (Rootguard, Heartwood Elk) use them up and heal or shield more for each. Only your own creatures use your Blossoms.
- New ability: Dewfall (Skill Card level 2, 1.5 sec cast, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Heals an ally for 1,012, 10% more for each of your Blossoms on them. Uses none.
- New ability: Fawn (Skill Card level 4, 1.5 sec cast, 8 sec cooldown, 3 mana points, 40 m range). A fawn heals an ally for 1,012, then bounces up to 3 times, each time to the lowest-health ally within 12 m it has not healed yet, healing 20% less with each bounce.
- New ability: Rootguard (Skill Card level 6, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Roots wrap an ally in a shield of 810, plus 405 for each of your Blossoms on them (used up), for 8 sec. When the shield is used up, every ally within 8 m of them gets a Blossom.
- New ability: Owl (Skill Card level 8, instant, 12 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). An owl watches over an ally for 15 sec. The first time they are hit, it dives: it heals them for 1,012, leaves a Blossom, and strikes the attacker, or the nearest enemy within 10 m, for 230 Nature damage (its own line on the damage meter: Owl's Strike). If nothing hits them, it lands and leaves a Blossom.
- New ability: Heartwood Elk (Skill Card level 10, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 3 mana points). An elk rises beside you and heals every ally within 10 m for 506, plus 304 for each of your Blossoms on them (used up). Full healing for up to 5 allies; with more, it is split evenly between them. Allies who had 3 Blossoms get Full Bloom: 10% less damage taken for 6 sec.
- New ability: Wild Herd (Skill Card level 12, instant, 120 sec cooldown, 3 mana points). Sends a Fawn at each of the 5 lowest-health allies within 40 m. Each is a full Fawn, with its bounces and Blossoms.
- New talent: Running Herd (row 1, 4 ranks, requires Fawn). Fawn heals 5 / 10 / 15% more. At full rank, every second Fawn bounces 2 more times.

### Nature tank: the Colossus (new playstyle: Colossus)
A melee tank that uses Rage. A huge giant woven of vines fights through you, one limb at a time; with Awaken, the whole giant rises behind you.
- Sap: a buff of up to 5 stacks that lasts 20 sec, refreshed whenever you gain more. Each stack heals you for 0.4% of your maximum health every second (its own line on the healing meter: Sap).
- New ability: Vine Fist (Skill Card level 2, instant, 3 sec cooldown, generates 20 Rage). A giant fist punches over your shoulder, dealing 460 Nature damage to the target and 138 to up to 2 more enemies within 4 m of it, with 4x threat. Gives 1 Sap.
- New ability: Grasp (Skill Card level 4, instant, 10 sec cooldown, 15 Rage, 25 m range). A giant hand rises under the target and drops it at your feet. It must attack you for 3 sec and is held in place for 1 sec (it can still attack).
- New ability: Stomp (Skill Card level 6, instant, 8 sec cooldown, 25 Rage). A giant foot slams down, dealing 368 Nature damage to every enemy within 8 m and making them attack you for 3 sec. Double damage to an enemy held by Grasp. Thorny roots stay in the cracks for 6 sec, dealing 276 Nature damage over that time to each enemy inside (its own line on the damage meter: Stomp Roots). Full damage to up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them. Gives 1 Sap, or 2 when it hits 3 or more enemies.
- New ability: Giant's Hands (Skill Card level 8, instant, 20 sec cooldown, 30 Rage). The giant's hands close around you, absorbing damage up to 30% of your maximum health for 8 sec. Each hit they absorb gives 1 Sap. When the shield breaks or runs out, the hands burst open for 368 Nature damage to every enemy within 6 m, with 6x threat (split past 5 enemies).
- New ability: Heartsap (Skill Card level 10, instant, 12 sec cooldown, 20 Rage). The giant's chest opens behind you and pours sap over you: uses all your Sap and heals you for 5% of your maximum health, plus 3% for each Sap (20% with 5).
- New ability: Awaken (Skill Card level 12, instant, 120 sec cooldown). The whole giant rises behind you for 12 sec. You take 15% less damage and gain twice the Sap. Vine Fist hits twice and slams the ground for 184 Nature damage to every enemy within 6 m (its own line on the damage meter: Giant's Slam), Stomp reaches 12 m, and Giant's Hands absorbs 50% more.
- New talent: Thick Sap (row 2, 4 ranks, requires Heartsap). Heartsap heals 5 / 10 / 15% more. At full rank, Heartsap leaves you 2 Sap.

### Nature archer: Venomstring (new playstyle: Venomstring)
A bow archer with three poisons. Its abilities cost no mana or Energy; Venom pays for the big shots. A woven snake coils around each of its arrows in flight.
- Poisons: each lasts 12 sec, benefits from Critical Strike and Haste, and drips in its colour on the enemy with every tick. When a poisoned enemy dies, each of your poisons on it jumps to the nearest enemy within 10 m with the time it had left.
- Two different poisons of yours on one enemy: all of your poisons on it deal 15% more damage. All three (Steeped): 30% more instead.
- Venom: a bar of up to 100, shown just above your action bar while Adder Arrow, Asp Arrow and Krait Arrow are all on your bars. While all three of your poisons are on enemies anywhere, you have Venom Flow and each tick of your poisons gives 3 Venom. Out of combat, Venom drains away.
- New ability: Fang Arrow (Skill Card level 2, instant). Deals 207 Nature damage. Every poison of yours on the target lasts 2 sec longer, and your next Adder Arrow is instant.
- New ability: Adder Arrow (Skill Card level 4, 1.5 sec cast). Puts Adder Venom on the target: 1,104 Nature damage over 12 sec.
- New ability: Asp Arrow (Skill Card level 6, instant). Puts Asp Venom on the target: 552 Nature damage over 12 sec, and it moves 20% slower.
- New ability: Krait Arrow (Skill Card level 8, instant). Puts Krait Venom on the target: 368 Nature damage over 12 sec. Your other poisons on it tick 25% faster.
- New ability: Venom Burst (Skill Card level 10, instant, 50 Venom). The target explodes, taking all the damage your poisons on it have left, 20% more for each different poison, and the poisons are removed. Enemies within 6 m take 50% of it (its own line on the damage meter; split past 5 enemies). On a Steeped target you get 20 Venom back.
- New ability: Spitting Volley (Skill Card level 12, instant, 30 Venom). Arrows rain down within 8 m of the target and put Adder Venom on every enemy there. Full damage on up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them.
- New ability: Bloat Arrow (Skill Card level 14, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 40 Venom). The target swells, and after 3 sec it bursts: every poison of yours on it is copied to each enemy within 8 m, with the time it has left.
- New ability: Coil Back (Skill Card level 16, instant, 20 sec cooldown). Leap 10 m backward, leaving a thorn trap where you stood for 8 sec. The first enemy to come within 2 m of it is held in place for 1 sec and gets Asp Venom.
- New ability: Venom Surge (Skill Card level 18, instant, 120 sec cooldown). For 15 sec your poisons go on at double strength, Venom Flow stays on even with one of your poisons missing, and Spitting Volley costs no Venom.
- New talent: Lingering Venom (row 2, 4 ranks, requires Venom Burst). Venom Burst deals 5 / 10 / 15% more damage. At full rank, each poison Venom Burst would remove has a 40% chance to stay on the target.

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: the Nature book's spellbook now shows four playstyles: Thornweaver, Shepherd, Colossus and Venomstring.
- New keywords with Shift explanations: Mulch, Blossom, Full Bloom, Sap, Venom, Venom Flow and Steeped.
- Changed: Rime Ward (shields you apply absorb more) now also strengthens Rootguard and Giant's Hands.
