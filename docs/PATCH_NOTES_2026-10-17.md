# Patch notes — 17 October 2026

## Fire

- New ability: Cooling Slag (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, 1.5 sec cast, card level 14). Pours molten slag on the ground within 5 m of the target. Enemies in it move 30% slower. After 2 sec the slag hardens, rooting every enemy still inside for 3 sec. Found in Fire's new Utility section.
- New ability: Bellows (2 mana, 25 sec cooldown, instant, card level 16). A blast of forge air throws every enemy within 6 m of you 8 m away. Bosses are too heavy to throw. Gives you a stack of Heat for each enemy thrown, up to 3.
- Changed: Furnace Breath, Cinder Step, Heat Sink and Cinderwing moved to Fire's Utility section.

## Frost

- Changed: Rimelock, Flash Freeze, Winter Hide, Ice Barrier and Snow Ward moved to Frost's new Utility section.

## Lightning

- New ability: Static Snare (20 Energy, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target for 2 sec. When that ends, the charge leaps once to the nearest other enemy within 8 m and roots it for 2 sec. Found in Lightning's new Utility section.
- New ability: Storm Front (20 Energy, 60 sec cooldown, instant, card level 22). You and every ally within 15 m move 30% faster for 6 sec. You gain 5 Energy for each ally it reaches.
- Changed: Flicker, used again within 4 sec, now snaps you back to where you flickered from, at no cost.
- Changed: Jolt, Flicker, Static Step, Thunder Strike, Faraday Guard, Ground Out and Recharge moved to Lightning's Utility section.

## Arcane

- New ability: Fixed Point (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target for 3 sec, plus 0.5 sec for each of your other Arcane spells on cooldown, up to 6 sec. Found in Arcane's new Utility section.
- Changed: Unweave, Phase Step and Interval Ward moved to Arcane's Utility section.

## Shadow

- New ability: Back on Your Feet (Strings Attached; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. The demon's Strings lift a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- New ability: Climb the String (1 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, 30 m, card level 16). A String hauls you to your friendly target, or to your enemy target if you have none, and sets you down 1.5 m short of it. On an ally, it stays tied to them as a loose String for 8 sec. Found in Shadow's new Utility section.
- Changed: Tangle, Hush and String Up moved to Shadow's new Utility section.

## Nature

- New ability: Rootsnare (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target and every enemy within 3 m of it for 3 sec, and leaves a patch of Mulch under the target.
- New ability: Deep Roots (1 mana, 60 sec cooldown, instant, card level 18). For 8 sec you take 15% less damage and gain 1 Sap every second. Moving ends it.
- New ability: Spore Sleep (2 mana, 30 sec cooldown, 2 sec cast, card level 20). Puts the target to sleep for 20 sec: it cannot move, attack or cast. Any damage wakes it, and when it wakes it gets your Asp Venom. Bosses and elites cannot be put to sleep.
- New ability: Leaf Cloak (no cost, 2 min cooldown, instant, card level 24). Your threat on every enemy within 30 m drops to nothing, and for 4 sec your damage makes no threat.
- New ability: Return to Bloom (Shepherd; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. Vines raise a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- Changed: the four new utility abilities sit in Nature's new Utility section. Any spec can use them; Rootsnare, Deep Roots and Spore Sleep also feed a Nature spec's own resource (Mulch, Sap or Asp Venom).
- Changed: Running Herd, Hunting Party, Thick Sap and Lingering Venom now have their own icons in the talent tree.

## Holy

- New ability: Binding Ring (2 mana, 30 sec cooldown, instant, 40 m, card level 14). A ring of light circles your friendly target (you, if you have none) for 8 sec. The first 3 enemies to come within 3 m of them are rooted for 3 sec. Found in Holy's new Utility section.
- New ability: Circle of Silence (2 mana, 25 sec cooldown, instant, 30 m, card level 18). A circle of silence settles within 5 m of the target for 4 sec. Every enemy casting inside it is interrupted, and so is any enemy that starts a cast inside it while it lasts. Each is prevented from casting again for 3 sec. Holy's interrupt; it shares the cooldown of every other interrupt.
- Changed: Steady Voice, Rest and Unfallen moved to Holy's Utility section.
- New ability: Hymn of Waking (Choir of One; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. Sings a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- New ability: Relight (Line of Light; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. A beam of light brings a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- Changed: every Holy talent now has its own icon in the talent tree (45 new pictures).

## Martial

- Removed: every Martial ability (16, including the starter abilities Heavy Blow, Headsman's Cut, Steady Shot, Stoneguard and Taunt) and their Skill Cards. The book stays on the shelf, empty, until it is rebuilt. Tanks now taunt with their own book's abilities.

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: only healers can revive. Holding F next to a downed ally no longer revives them, and the Revive Downed Ally keybind is gone. A downed ally can be brought back in combat by Last Verse or Not Finished With You, and every healer spec now has an out-of-combat revive that also works on allies who have bled out.
- Changed: every book except Martial has a new Utility section in its spellbook. It holds the book's crowd control, roots, interrupts, movement, party buffs and defensives that work without the rest of a spec's kit, so any spec of that book can find them in one place.
- Changed: Splintering, Permafrost, Deep Freeze and Ice Floe now show in Frost blue on the damage meter, and Lifesteal in red, instead of plain grey.
- Changed: menus, the bags, the bank, the runes panel and the action bar now refresh once per frame when many things change at once, instead of once per change. Large changes (such as learning many abilities at once) no longer freeze the game.
- Changed: large groups of enemies cost less performance to keep apart.

## Fixes

- Fixed: talent points that older saves had in four empty Nature talent slots counted towards Running Herd, Hunting Party, Thick Sap and Lingering Venom, even without their ability learned. Those points are refunded.
- Fixed: a talent that only works with one ability no longer keeps its points when a save is loaded without that ability learned; the points are refunded.
- Fixed: the tooltips of Vine Fist, Stomp and Giant's Hands now take their Sap amounts from the game's numbers.
