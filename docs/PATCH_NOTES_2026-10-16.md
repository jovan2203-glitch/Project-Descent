# Patch notes — 16 October 2026

Heals and damage are given at level 50 before gear. Mana costs are in mana points; one point is 10% of your base mana.

## Shadow

### New talents
- New talent: Deep Call (2 ranks, row 6, requires Beckon). While your Breach under the target is open, Beckon takes 0.25 / 0.5 sec less to cast (1.75 / 1.5 sec instead of 2 sec).
- New talent: Ill Will (3 ranks, row 7). Critical strikes of your damage over time effects deal 5 / 10 / 15% more damage.
- New talent: Hard Pull (2 ranks, row 8, requires Tug). Tug makes the ally's Strings heal faster for 1 / 2 sec longer (5 / 6 sec instead of 4 sec).
- New talent: Wide Pack (2 ranks, row 9, requires Run Them Down). During Run Them Down, each bite of your Hounds hits 1 / 2 more enemies (3 / 4 instead of 2).

### Talent tree
- Moved: Small Change moved from row 8 to row 5, straight below Interest. Points already spent in it are kept.

## Holy

### The Holy book starts over
- Removed: every Holy ability: Heal, Dawnlight, Sunlance, Oath Strike, Radiant Arrow, Shield of Dawn and Sacred Bulwark, along with their Skill Cards. Skill Cards you owned for them are gone. The book is being rebuilt one playstyle at a time; the healer comes first.
- Removed: Sacred Bulwark's retaliation burst when you are hit or block an attack.
- Changed: the starter heal every character has is now Psalm (2 sec cast, 2 mana points, 1,012 heal) instead of Heal (2.2 sec cast, 2 mana points, 1,012 heal). It sits in the fourth slot of a new action bar.
- Changed: Snow Ward, Cupped Hands and the Rune of the Frozen Aegis are still sized from the old Heal spell's heal. Their numbers do not change.

### Holy healer: the Hymns (new playstyle: Choir of One)
A new way to play the Holy book: a healer who sings. Its seventeen spells need no particular weapon.

#### Hymns
- You sing one of three Hymns: Mercy, Chorus or Vigil. You sing none until you first choose one. A ring of glowing glyphs turns around you in your Hymn's colour: gold for Mercy, white for Chorus, blue for Vigil. Your party sees it too.
- Mercy: your heals on a single ally (Psalm, Outpour, Lift, Refrain, Antiphon) heal 20% more.
- Chorus: those heals, except Refrain's, also heal up to 2 other allies within 8 m of the one you healed, for 30% of the heal. This has its own line on the healing meter: Chorus.
- Vigil: allies within 15 m of you, you included, take 5% less damage, and each of your heals leaves a shield worth 10% of the heal on whoever it healed, for 3 sec.
- New ability: Sing Mercy (Skill Card level 2, instant, no cost). You start singing Mercy.
- New ability: Sing Chorus (Skill Card level 8, instant, no cost). You start singing Chorus.
- New ability: Sing Vigil (Skill Card level 14, instant, no cost). You start singing Vigil.
  - The three Sing abilities trigger the global cooldown, cannot be used while casting, and cannot be used for the Hymn you already sing.

#### Verses
- Your healer spells give Verses, up to 6. Verses past 6 are wasted.
- Each Verse suits one Hymn: heals on a single ally give Mercy Verses, heals on several allies give Chorus Verses, shields and less damage taken give Vigil Verses.
- Your Verses are shown as 6 pips under your resource bars, each in the colour of its Hymn, next to the name of the Hymn you sing. The Sing, Reprise and Last Verse buttons show how many you hold.
- Out of combat, one Verse fades every 2 sec.
- Singing a Hymn with 6 Verses makes the Hymn you enter boom, and spends all your Verses.
- Singing a Hymn with fewer than 6 costs you all your Verses. Your first Sing never does.

#### Booms
- Benediction (entering Mercy): your friendly target, or you if you have none, is healed for 3,036.
- Chorale (entering Chorus): every ally within 30 m, you included, is healed for 1,012.
- Bulwark of Song (entering Vigil): allies within 15 m of you take 30% less damage for 5 sec.
- A boom is 10% stronger for each spent Verse of its own Hymn, up to 60% with six. Bulwark of Song lasts 0.5 sec longer for each instead (up to 8 sec).
- Full Choir: when your last three booms were Mercy, Chorus and Vigil in any order, the third is 50% stronger (Bulwark of Song lasts 50% longer) and gives you 2 Verses of its Hymn back. The count then starts over.
- Each boom is a column of light in its Hymn's colour, which your party sees too. Benediction and Chorale have their own lines on the healing meter.

#### New abilities
- New ability: Psalm (every character has it, 2 sec cast, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 1,012. Gives 1 Mercy Verse.
- New ability: Lift (Skill Card level 4, instant, 8 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 506. Gives 1 Mercy Verse.
- New ability: Refrain (Skill Card level 6, instant, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 1,214 over 12 sec, 101 every second. Haste makes it heal more often. Using it on them again starts it over. Gives 1 Mercy Verse.
- New ability: Shelter (Skill Card level 10, instant, 12 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Shields your friendly target, or you if you have none, absorbing 1,214 damage for 8 sec. Gives 1 Vigil Verse.
- New ability: Outpour (Skill Card level 12, 3 sec cast, 4 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 2,226. Gives 2 Mercy Verses.
- New ability: Canticle (Skill Card level 16, 2.5 sec cast, 12 sec cooldown, 3 mana points). Heals up to 5 injured allies within 30 m of you, you included, for 607 each, lowest health first. Gives 2 Chorus Verses.
- New ability: Antiphon (Skill Card level 18, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target for 810, then you for half of that. With no friendly target it heals only you, for 810. Gives 1 Chorus Verse.
- New ability: High Note (Skill Card level 20, 1.5 sec cast, 2 mana points, 30 m range). Deals 460 Holy damage to your target. Your lowest-health ally within 30 m of you, you included, is healed for 50% of the damage dealt. Gives 1 Verse of the Hymn you sing (a Mercy Verse if you sing none).
- New ability: Steady Voice (Skill Card level 22, instant, 30 sec cooldown, 2 mana points). Every ally within 15 m of you, you included, takes 10% less damage for 6 sec. Gives 2 Vigil Verses.
- New ability: Exultation (Skill Card level 24, 2 sec cast, no cooldown, 4 mana points). Heals every ally within 20 m of you, you included, for 455. Gives 1 Chorus Verse.
- New ability: Swell (Skill Card level 28, instant, 60 sec cooldown, no cost, off the global cooldown). For 10 sec, everything that gives you Verses gives 1 more.
- New ability: Reprise (Skill Card level 32, instant, no cooldown, costs 3 Verses). Repeats your last boom at 40% of its strength, where you are now. Usable within 6 sec of a boom. It does not count as a boom for Full Choir.
- New ability: Rest (Skill Card level 36, instant, 60 sec cooldown, no cost). For 6 sec you take 40% less damage, and singing a Hymn without a boom keeps all your Verses.
- New ability: Last Verse (Skill Card level 44, instant, 3 min cooldown, no cost, 30 m range). Lifts the nearest downed ally back onto their feet with 30% health. Needs 6 Verses and spends them all.

#### New talents
- New talent: Mercy's Ear (2 ranks, row 1). Healing an ally below 35% health gives you a Mercy Verse, at most once every 6 / 3 sec.
- New talent: Many Voices (2 ranks, row 1). A heal of yours that lands on 3 or more allies at once gives you a Chorus Verse, at most once every 6 / 3 sec. Chorus's extra heals count.
- New talent: Watchful (2 ranks, row 1). An ally standing in your Vigil who takes a hit of 10% of their maximum health or more gives you a Vigil Verse, at most once every 6 / 3 sec.
- New talent: Key Change (3 ranks, row 2). Singing a Hymn without a boom keeps 1 / 2 / 3 of your Verses. A boom still spends them all.
- New talent: Open Doors (4 ranks, row 2, requires Shelter). Shelter's shield absorbs 10 / 20 / 30 / 40% more (1,335 / 1,457 / 1,578 / 1,700). At 4 ranks, Shelter's cooldown is 4 sec instead of 12 sec.

#### New runes
- New rune: Rune of Resounding (Epic). Your booms also deal 276 Holy damage to enemies within 8 m: Benediction around the ally it heals, Chorale and Bulwark of Song around you. It hits up to 5 enemies at full damage; with more, the damage is split evenly between them. It has its own line on the damage meter.
- New rune: Rune of the Overture (Epic). After a boom, your next spell with a cast time within 3 sec is instant.

#### In a party
- Your heals, shields and less damage taken reach party members on their own machines, like every other heal.
- "Hymn", "Verse" and "Full Choir" are keywords: they are shown in orange and explained when you hold Shift.
