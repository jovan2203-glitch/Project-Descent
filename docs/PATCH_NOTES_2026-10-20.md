# Patch notes — 20 October 2026 (0.0.16)

Damage is given at level 50 before gear. Mana costs are in mana points; one point is 10% of your base mana. These notes cover everything since 0.0.15, including the changes of 17 October.

## Fire

- New ability: Cooling Slag (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, 1.5 sec cast, card level 14). Pours molten slag on the ground within 5 m of the target. Enemies in it move 30% slower. After 2 sec the slag hardens, rooting every enemy still inside for 3 sec. Found in Fire's new Utility section.
- New ability: Bellows (2 mana, 25 sec cooldown, instant, card level 16). A blast of forge air throws every enemy within 6 m of you 8 m away. Bosses are too heavy to throw. Gives you a stack of Heat for each enemy thrown, up to 3.
- Moved: Furnace Breath, Cinder Step, Heat Sink and Cinderwing to Fire's Utility section.

## Frost

- Moved: Rimelock, Flash Freeze, Winter Hide, Ice Barrier and Snow Ward to Frost's new Utility section.

## Lightning

- New ability: Static Snare (20 Energy, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target for 2 sec. When that ends, the charge leaps once to the nearest other enemy within 8 m and roots it for 2 sec. Found in Lightning's new Utility section.
- New ability: Storm Front (20 Energy, 60 sec cooldown, instant, card level 22). You and every ally within 15 m move 30% faster for 6 sec. You gain 5 Energy for each ally it reaches.
- Changed: Flicker, used again within 4 sec, now snaps you back to where you flickered from, at no cost.
- Moved: Jolt, Flicker, Static Step, Thunder Strike, Faraday Guard, Ground Out and Recharge to Lightning's Utility section.

## Arcane

- New ability: Fixed Point (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target for 3 sec, plus 0.5 sec for each of your other Arcane spells on cooldown, up to 6 sec. Found in Arcane's new Utility section.
- Moved: Unweave, Phase Step and Interval Ward to Arcane's Utility section.

## Shadow

- New ability: Back on Your Feet (Strings Attached; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. The demon's Strings lift a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- New ability: Climb the String (1 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, 30 m, card level 16). A String hauls you to your friendly target, or to your enemy target if you have none, and sets you down 1.5 m short of it. On an ally, it stays tied to them as a loose String for 8 sec. Found in Shadow's new Utility section.
- Moved: Tangle, Hush and String Up to Shadow's new Utility section.

## Nature

- New ability: Rootsnare (2 mana, 20 sec cooldown, instant, card level 14). Roots the target and every enemy within 3 m of it for 3 sec, and leaves a patch of Mulch under the target.
- New ability: Deep Roots (1 mana, 60 sec cooldown, instant, card level 18). For 8 sec you take 15% less damage and gain 1 Sap every second. Moving ends it.
- New ability: Spore Sleep (2 mana, 30 sec cooldown, 2 sec cast, card level 20). Puts the target to sleep for 20 sec: it cannot move, attack or cast. Any damage wakes it, and when it wakes it gets your Asp Venom. Bosses and elites cannot be put to sleep.
- New ability: Leaf Cloak (no cost, 2 min cooldown, instant, card level 24). Your threat on every enemy within 30 m drops to nothing, and for 4 sec your damage makes no threat.
- New ability: Return to Bloom (Shepherd; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. Vines raise a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- Changed: Rootsnare, Deep Roots, Spore Sleep and Leaf Cloak sit in Nature's new Utility section. Any spec can use them; Rootsnare, Deep Roots and Spore Sleep also feed a Nature spec's own resource (Mulch, Sap or Asp Venom).
- New talent: Rich Soil (Thornweaver; row 3, 3 ranks). Each patch of mulch adds 10 / 20 / 30% more to the per-patch damage of Mossback and Choking Vipers.
- New talent: Compost (Thornweaver; row 5, 1 rank, requires Thornshot). Every 2nd Thornshot that lands leaves a patch of mulch, instead of every 3rd. Maxing it opens Bramble Burst.
- New talent: Bramble Burst (Thornweaver; row 6, 3 ranks, requires Thornshot). Thornburst deals 10 / 20 / 30% more damage. At full rank, it reaches 5 m from each patch instead of 4 m.
- New talent: Fertile Ground (Thornweaver; row 7, 2 ranks, requires Choking Vipers). An enemy that dies with your Vipers on it has a 50 / 100% chance to leave a patch of mulch where it fell.
- New talent: Second Growth (Thornweaver; row 8, 2 ranks, requires Mossback). Mossback has a 15 / 30% chance to leave a patch of mulch where it strikes.
- New talent: Circling Hawk (Thornweaver; row 8, 2 ranks, requires Thornwing). An ability that uses 4 or more patches cuts Thornwing's cooldown by 3 / 6 sec.
- New talent: Overgrown (Thornweaver; row 10, 5 ranks). Your Nature spells deal 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 / 1.6 / 2% more damage for each patch of mulch you have (10% with 5 patches at full rank).
- New talent: Old Bark (Shepherd; row 3, 3 ranks, requires Rootguard). Rootguard's shield absorbs 10 / 20 / 30% more.
- New talent: Morning Dew (Shepherd; row 5, 2 ranks, requires Dewfall). Dewfall heals 15 / 20% more for each Blossom, instead of 10%.
- New talent: Return Flight (Shepherd; row 6, 2 ranks, requires Owl). After its dive, the Owl keeps watching for the time it had left and can dive once more at 30 / 60% strength (heal and strike). The second watch leaves no Blossom when it lands.
- New talent: Deep Bloom (Shepherd; row 7, 2 ranks, requires Heartwood Elk). Full Bloom lasts 2 / 4 sec longer.
- New talent: Quick Return (Shepherd; row 8, 2 ranks, requires Return to Bloom). Return to Bloom's cast is 2 / 4 sec shorter.
- New talent: Big Herd (Shepherd; row 8, 2 ranks, requires Wild Herd). Wild Herd's cooldown is 15 / 30 sec shorter.
- New talent: Evergreen (Shepherd; row 10, 5 ranks). Each of your Blossoms heals its ally for 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.15 / 0.2 / 0.25% of their maximum health every second (its own line on the healing meter: Evergreen).
- New talent: Rising Sap (Colossus; row 4, 3 ranks). Each Sap heals you for 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7% of your maximum health per second, instead of 0.4%. Also counts for the Sap from Deep Roots.
- New talent: Crushing Grip (Colossus; row 5, 3 ranks, requires Giant's Hands). The burst of Giant's Hands deals 5 / 10 / 15% more damage for each hit the Hands absorbed, up to 5 hits.
- New talent: Broad Fist (Colossus; row 6, 2 ranks, requires Vine Fist). Vine Fist's splash hits 1 / 2 more enemies.
- New talent: Spreading Roots (Colossus; row 7, 2 ranks, requires Stomp). Stomp Roots last 2 / 4 sec longer and reach 1 / 2 m further, with the same damage per second.
- New talent: Firm Hold (Colossus; row 9, 2 ranks, requires Grasp). Grasp holds the enemy 0.5 / 1 sec longer and gives 5 / 10 Fury back.
- New talent: Wide Awake (Colossus; row 9, 2 ranks, requires Awaken). Awaken lasts 3 / 6 sec longer.
- New talent: Old Growth (Colossus; row 10, 5 ranks). Each Sap you have cuts the damage you take by 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 / 1.6 / 2% (10% with 5 Sap at full rank).
- New talent: Long Fangs (Venomstring; row 1, 2 ranks, requires Fang Arrow). Fang Arrow adds 3 / 4 sec to your poisons on the target, instead of 2.
- New talent: Hungry Venom (Venomstring; row 3, 2 ranks). When a poisoned enemy dies, your poisons jump up to 15 / 20 m instead of 10 m, and deal 10 / 20% more damage after jumping (once, however often they jump).
- New talent: Spring Trap (Venomstring; row 4, 2 ranks, requires Coil Back). Coil Back's thorn trap catches up to 2 / 3 enemies at once.
- New talent: Soaked Through (Venomstring; row 9, 5 ranks). Steeped makes your poisons deal 32 / 34 / 36 / 38 / 40% more, instead of 30%.
- New talent: Rough Bark (open; row 1, 3 ranks). 3 / 6 / 9% more Armor.
- New talent: Green Thumb (open; row 2, 3 ranks). Your heals over time heal 4 / 8 / 12% more.
- New talent: Undergrowth (open; row 3, 3 ranks). Your damage over time deals 4 / 8 / 12% more to an enemy held by your own root, hold or slow.

## Holy

- New ability: Binding Ring (2 mana, 30 sec cooldown, instant, 40 m, card level 14). A ring of light circles your friendly target (you, if you have none) for 8 sec. The first 3 enemies to come within 3 m of them are rooted for 3 sec. Found in Holy's new Utility section.
- New ability: Circle of Silence (2 mana, 25 sec cooldown, instant, 30 m, card level 18). A circle of silence settles within 5 m of the target for 4 sec. Every enemy casting inside it is interrupted, and so is any enemy that starts a cast inside it while it lasts. Each is prevented from casting again for 3 sec. Holy's interrupt; it shares the cooldown of every other interrupt.
- New ability: Hymn of Waking (Choir of One; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. Sings a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- New ability: Relight (Line of Light; 4 mana, 8 sec cast, card level 20). Out of combat only. A beam of light brings a fallen ally within 30 m back up where they fell, with 50% health and 50% mana.
- Moved: Steady Voice, Rest and Unfallen to Holy's Utility section.
- Changed: every Holy talent has its own icon in the talent tree (45 new pictures).

## Martial

The Martial book is rebuilt with three specs and a Utility section. Every ability is Physical and scales like an ability. Its talents come in a later patch.

- Removed: the 16 old Martial abilities (including the starter abilities Heavy Blow, Headsman's Cut, Steady Shot, Stoneguard and Taunt) and their Skill Cards. Tanks taunt with their own book's abilities.

### The Wheel (two-handed weapons, Fury)

- New: the Wheel. Four glowing two-handers float on your back and turn in a fixed order: Greataxe, Greatsword, Halberd, Maul, then back to the Greataxe. You fight with the one in your hands; the weapon you have equipped only gives stats, and any melee weapon works.
- New: each held weapon takes 4 uses. Every ability you use counts, from any book, except abilities off the global cooldown and trinkets. After the 4th use it breaks and the next weapon flies into your hands; the broken one reforms at the back of the line. Leaving combat makes the held weapon whole again. Out of combat, click the Wheel strip above the action bar to turn to the next weapon and choose your opening weapon.
- New: Cracks. Fillers add 1 Crack and Heavy Fillers 2 to the held weapon (no cap). They are lost if the weapon breaks without a Finisher.
- New ability: Filler, Heavy Filler, Weapon Art and Finisher. Four bar buttons that take the held weapon's form. The spellbook lists each weapon with a dropdown of its four moves.
- New ability: Greataxe (card level 2). While held, your ability hits also strike 1 enemy within 5 m of the target for 25% of the damage (its own line on the damage meter: Axe Cleave).
  - Hack (Filler; no cost, generates 20 Fury). 276 damage, and 138 to up to 2 more enemies within 5 m of the target.
  - Chop (Heavy Filler; 30 Fury). 368 damage, and 184 to up to 2 more enemies within 5 m of the target.
  - Rush (Weapon Art; 15 sec cooldown). Dash up to 12 m forward, through enemies.
  - Shrapnel (Finisher). The axe breaks into shards around you, dealing 92 damage plus 276 per Crack to every enemy within 8 m. Full damage to up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them.
- New ability: Greatsword (card level 4). While held, you have 10% more critical strike chance.
  - Slash (Filler; no cost, generates 20 Fury). 322 damage.
  - Lunge (Heavy Filler; 30 Fury). 437 damage.
  - Blade Guard (Weapon Art; 45 sec cooldown). You take 40% less damage for 4 sec.
  - Shard Lance (Finisher). Every shard of the sword drives into the target, dealing 110 damage plus 331 per Crack.
- New ability: Halberd (card level 6). While held, your ability hits have a 20% chance to strike again for the same damage (its own line on the damage meter: Second Hit).
  - Jab (Filler; no cost, generates 30 Fury). 276 damage to up to 3 enemies in front of you within 5 m.
  - Hook (Heavy Filler; 20 Fury). 368 damage to up to 3 enemies in front of you within 5 m.
  - Drag In (Weapon Art; 20 sec cooldown, 12 m). Hook one enemy and drag it to you. It does not taunt.
  - Flying Head (Finisher). The head snaps off and hits the target for 92 damage plus 276 per Crack, then bounces to 3 more enemies, each within 8 m of the last, for 50% of that.
- New ability: Maul (card level 8). While held, your ability hits build Stagger on the target. A full Stagger knocks it down for 1 sec, then starts over. An enemy you haven't hit for 8 sec loses its Stagger. Bosses build no Stagger.
  - Thud (Filler; no cost, generates 20 Fury). 276 damage and extra Stagger.
  - Crush (Heavy Filler; 30 Fury). 368 damage and extra Stagger.
  - Earthshake (Weapon Art; 30 sec cooldown). Knocks down every enemy within 6 m for 1.5 sec. Not bosses.
  - Fault Line (Finisher). A shockwave in an 8 m cone in front of you deals 92 damage plus 276 per Crack, with extra Stagger. Full damage to up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them.

### Brawler (tank, Fury)

A pugilist who takes the hit now and pays for it later. No Brawler ability works with a shield in the off hand; two-handers, dual wielding, other off hands and an empty hand all work.

- New: Bruise. 40% of every hit you take is not dealt at once: it goes into Bruise and is dealt to you evenly over 6 sec. New damage adds to it and starts the 6 sec over with the total left. Each Bruise tick gives you Fury. The health bar shows your Bruise as a darker segment.
- New: the chain. Punch, Kick and Elbow go into a chain of 3. When the third lands, a move goes off on top of it and the chain clears; 5 sec without a strike also clears it. The last strike picks the move, and three different strikes make its strong version. Orbs over your back (red Punch, green Kick, blue Elbow, white Catch) and the chain strip above the action bar show the chain, and with 2 strikes in, each strike button shows the move it would finish.
- New ability: Punch, Kick and Elbow (card level 2; no cost, generate 10 Fury, 1 sec global cooldown). Each deals 138 damage with 4x threat and goes into the chain.
  - Ending with Punch: Rapid Fists, 207 damage with 5x threat. All different: Haymaker, 506 damage, spending 30 Fury (without it, Rapid Fists).
  - Ending with Kick: Sweep, 184 damage to every enemy within 5 m with 5x threat. All different: Roundhouse, 230 damage to every enemy within 6 m, knocking them down for 1 sec (not bosses). Full damage to up to 5 enemies; with more, it is split evenly between them.
  - Ending with Elbow: Clinch, you take 30% less damage from the target for 4 sec. All different: Shake Off, clears 30% of your Bruise.
- New ability: Fighting Style (card level 2; no cost, off the global cooldown). Switches your strikes between Armed and Bare Hands. It only changes how they look.
- New ability: Catch (card level 4; no cost, 1 sec global cooldown). Usable only while your target is about to hit you in melee. You catch that hit: all of it lands now, skipping Bruise. Catch clears your chain and starts a new one. The last strike of a Catch chain decides the move:
  - Turn It Around (ends with Punch): the caught hit's full damage goes back into the target, with 4x threat.
  - Throw (ends with Kick): throws the enemy 6 m away for 276 damage; it and enemies within 3 m of where it lands are knocked down for 1 sec. On a boss: Reversal, 460 damage instead.
  - Takedown (ends with Elbow): the enemy is on the floor for 3 sec. On a boss: clears Bruise equal to the caught hit instead.
- New ability: Come On (card level 6; 8 sec cooldown, 25 m). The Brawler's taunt: the target must attack you for 3 sec.
- New ability: Tough It Out (card level 8; 40 Fury, 30 sec cooldown). For 5 sec your Bruise ticks deal no damage and give twice the Fury.
- New ability: One More (card level 10; 25 Fury, 15 sec cooldown, 1.5 sec global cooldown). Repeats the last chain move you made, Catch moves included, with the same caught hit. A repeated Haymaker also spends its own 30 Fury. Your chain stays as it is.

### Rogue (two one-handed weapons, Energy)

Every Rogue ability needs a one-handed melee weapon in each hand. Quick hits build Flow; you choose when to spend it on a Kill window.

- New: Flow. A buff of up to 10 stacks, each giving 1% more damage. Every Rogue ability that hits adds Flow (auto attacks don't). A hit from a different range than your last one (a throw after a melee hit, or the other way round) gives 1 more. After 3 sec without a hit it drops by 1 every second. The party sees it as a glow around you.
- New: stuck daggers. Thrown daggers stick in the target, up to 3 per enemy, for 20 sec; they are yours only and the party sees them. Melee abilities that rip them out deal 115 damage for each (its own line on the damage meter: Ripped Out).
- New ability: Cut (card level 2; 20 Energy). In melee, 253 damage with both blades. From 5 to 25 m, a thrown dagger for 207 damage that sticks. +1 Flow.
- New ability: Go In (comes with Cut; off the global cooldown, needs 5 Flow). Spends all your Flow and opens a Kill window of 1 sec per stack. During it, your builders take their Kill forms, each of your crits gives 10 Energy back, and no Flow builds. Each Kill form that differs from the one before it adds 0.5 sec to the window, up to 5 sec in all.
  - Gut (Cut's Kill form; 20 Energy): 460 damage; thrown, 368 damage and it sticks.
  - Through and Through (Twin Stab's; 30 Energy, no cooldown): 414 damage from each hand, rips out every stuck dagger.
  - Red Whirl (Whirl's; 35 Energy): 276 damage to every enemy within 5 m; with nobody that close, a Knife Spread at 230 damage per dagger.
  - Back Flip (Kick Off's; 15 Energy, shares its cooldown): 276 damage, flip back 8 m and throw 3 daggers into the target for 138 damage each, which stick.
  - Drive Through (Dart In's; 15 Energy, shares its cooldown): dash through the target for 368 damage, rip out one stuck dagger and reset Kick Off.
- New ability: Whirl (card level 4; 35 Energy). 161 damage to every enemy within 5 m, ripping out the daggers of every enemy hit (split past 5 enemies). With nobody that close, a Knife Spread: a dagger into each of up to 3 enemies in front of you for 138 damage, which sticks. +1 Flow per enemy hit, up to 3.
- New ability: Dart In (card level 6; 15 Energy, 10 sec cooldown, 15 m). Dash to the target for 230 damage and rip out one stuck dagger. +1 Flow.
- New ability: Kick Off (card level 8; 15 Energy, 10 sec cooldown). Kick the target for 184 damage and flip back 8 m. Your next throw within 4 sec throws two daggers. +1 Flow.
- New ability: Twin Stab (card level 10; 30 Energy, 8 sec cooldown). 230 damage from each hand, ripping out every stuck dagger. +2 Flow.
- New ability: Close It Out (card level 12; 20 Energy, Kill window only). Ends the Kill window for 184 damage per second it had left.
- New ability: Roll With It (card level 14; 25 sec cooldown, off the global cooldown). Spends 3 Flow: you take 40% less damage for 3 sec.
- New ability: Hit Your Stride (card level 16; 2 min cooldown, off the global cooldown). Sets your Flow to 10 and opens a Kill window 50% longer (15 sec).
- New ability: Snatch (card level 18; 15 Energy, 20 sec cooldown, melee). Lifts something off the target's belt and uses it at once, one of four at random. No Flow.
  - Flash Powder: enemies within 8 m in front of you are Blinded for 2 sec: they can't attack, move or cast. Not bosses.
  - Whetstone: your next 3 hits deal 50% more damage.
  - Smoke Flask: drops your threat on every enemy and gives you 20% dodge for 4 sec.
  - Oil Jar: a 4 m burning pool under the target for 6 sec, dealing 69 Fire damage every second to each enemy inside (split past 5 enemies; its own line on the damage meter: Oil Jar).

### Utility

No cost, cooldowns only. Each needs a melee weapon.

- New ability: Pin the Shadow (card level 14; 20 sec cooldown, 30 m). Martial's root. A thrown knife pins the enemy's shadow to the floor: it is rooted until it has taken 3 direct hits, from anyone (damage over time doesn't count), or 8 sec pass.
- New ability: Over the Shoulder (card level 18; 20 sec cooldown, 10 m). Vault over the target and land behind it. Your next hit within 5 sec is a sure critical strike.
- New ability: Play Dead (card level 22; 90 sec cooldown, off the global cooldown). You drop to the floor and every enemy forgets you for 3 sec: your threat is dropped and they turn to someone else, or stand over you if nobody is left. Using any ability or attacking ends it.

## Systems & Interface

- New: melee tells. Every enemy melee hit (trash, elites and bosses' attacks) now shows a short, bright tell 0.6 sec before it lands; Hasted elites and Frenzy shorten it. Enemies hit just as often as before. The tell is not a cast: interrupts don't stop it, but stuns and knockdowns do. The whole party sees it.
- Changed: Rage is now called Fury everywhere: the bar, tooltips, talents and Pacts.
- Changed: only healers can revive. Holding F next to a downed ally no longer revives them, and the Revive Downed Ally keybind is gone. A downed ally can be brought back in combat by Last Verse or Not Finished With You, and every healer spec now has an out-of-combat revive that also works on allies who have bled out.
- Changed: every book has a Utility section in its spellbook. It holds the book's crowd control, roots, interrupts, movement, party buffs and defensives that work without the rest of a spec's kit, so any spec of that book can find them in one place.
- New: the Wheel strip (the Wheel's weapons in order, the held one with its uses and Cracks), the chain strip (the Brawler's chain) and the Bruise segment on the health bar, above the action bar.
- Changed: Splintering, Permafrost, Deep Freeze and Ice Floe now show in Frost blue on the damage meter, and Lifesteal in red, instead of plain grey.
- Changed: menus, the bags, the bank, the runes panel and the action bar now refresh once per frame when many things change at once, instead of once per change. Large changes (such as learning many abilities at once) no longer freeze the game.
- Changed: large groups of enemies cost less performance to keep apart.

## Fixes

- Fixed: talent points that older saves had in four empty Nature talent slots counted towards Running Herd, Hunting Party, Thick Sap and Lingering Venom, even without their ability learned. Those points are refunded.
- Fixed: a talent that only works with one ability no longer keeps its points when a save is loaded without that ability learned; the points are refunded.
- Fixed: the tooltips of Vine Fist, Stomp and Giant's Hands now take their Sap amounts from the game's numbers.
