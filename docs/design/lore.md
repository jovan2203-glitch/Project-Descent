# Lore

Flavor first: the world explains the systems; no long story yet.

## The First Grimoire
Long ago all magic and martial skill was written in one book, the First Grimoire. Whoever read it could do anything. To stop one person holding all of it, it was torn into eight parts: the eight books. The pages were scattered and sank into the deep places under the world: **the Deep**.

## Binders (the players)
A Binder can bind loose pages into a personal grimoire: your spellbook.
- Leveling is finding pages and learning to read them; spending points is binding a page into your book.
- A book only holds so much, which is the point cap.
- Respec is unbinding and rebinding pages: free when safe, never in the Deep.

## The Bindery (hub)
The castle courtyard (the Training Room) is the Bindery, the last safe place above the Deep. Binders gather, train and form parties there; the lobby podiums sit here.

## The Deep
- **Leveling dungeons**: shallow parts of the Deep where loose pages drift.
  - **Zombies** are fallen Binders who got lost down there.
  - **Goblins** are scavengers stealing pages.
- **Endgame difficulty tiers**: going deeper. Each tier is a lower layer, where pages are older and stronger and the Unbound are more dangerous.
- **Raids**: the great chapters, places where a large part of the Grimoire wrote itself into a whole world. Each raid is themed on a book or a mix of books.

## Systems in-world
- **Runes**: marks left by past Binders in the margins. Rune Shards are what's left when you break down their gear.
- **Pacts**: bargains with something in the Deep. You trade strength for risk; breaking one leaves a mark until you leave.
- **Prestige (Rebinding)**: you give your pages back to the Deep and start a new book. Each rebinding leaves a permanent mark in the margins: the Prestige reward.
