# Patch notes — 12 October 2026

## Shadow healer: the Strings
A new way to play the Shadow book: a healer. A demon floats behind you and holds your allies up on puppet Strings. It has nineteen new spells. None of them needs a particular weapon. Mana costs below are in mana points; one point is 10% of your base mana.

### The Strings
- The demon has 6 Strings, for allies and enemies together. Each one is in your hand, tied to someone, or used up and recovering.
- The ability String shows the Strings in your hand as its charges.
- A String on an ally heals it every second for 15 sec (32 per heal at level 50 before gear). Haste makes the Strings heal more often.
- Up to 3 Strings go on one ally. Each one heals, so three heal three times as much. Tying another String to an ally starts the 15 sec of all its Strings over.
- A String on an enemy lasts 10 sec. An enemy holds one String.
- An ally on a String has a buff named String. Its stacks show how many Strings, and it shows the time they have left. Each healer's Strings are a buff of their own.
- If you die or go down, every String lets go.
- The Strings' heals have their own line on the healing meter: String.
- "Strings" and "Crossed Strings" are keywords: they are shown in orange and explained when you hold Shift.

### Getting Strings back
- A String that runs out is back in your hand at once. So is one whose target dies or is more than 60 m from you.
- A String is used up when you tie it to an ally that already has 3, or to an enemy that already has one. It only starts their time over. Pull Taut uses one up as well.
- Used-up Strings come back one at a time, 10 sec each.

### Shared heals
- When one of your spells or abilities heals an ally on a String, every other ally on one of your Strings is healed for 30% of that heal.
- This applies to every spell and ability of yours that heals, from any book.
- It does not apply to the heals of the Strings themselves, Cat's Cradle, Pull Taut or Cut the Strings, nor to Lifesteal and heals from talents and runes.
- The shared heal has its own line on the healing meter: Shared along the Strings.

### Crossed Strings
- Tying a String to an enemy crosses it with the ally you tied a String to last, if that ally still has one.
- The ally takes 3% less damage of every kind. The damage it is spared is dealt to the enemy as Shadow damage of yours. It cannot critically strike and has its own line on the damage meter: Crossed Strings.
- The crossing breaks when the two are more than 30 m apart; the enemy's String is then back in your hand. It also ends when the ally's Strings are gone, when the enemy's String runs out and when the enemy dies.
- An ally can be crossed with one enemy and an enemy with one ally. A newer crossing replaces the older one.
- The String on the enemy is one of your 6.

### New abilities
- New ability: String (Skill Card level 4, instant, no cost, no cooldown). Ties one String from your hand to your friendly target. With an enemy targeted it goes on that enemy; with no target it goes on you. 40 m range.
  - It uses only half a global cooldown.
  - It cannot be used with no String in your hand.
- New ability: Patch Up (Skill Card level 6, 2 sec cast, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 828 at level 50 before gear. It needs no String.
- New ability: Hoist (Skill Card level 8, 2 sec cast, 3 mana points, 40 m range). Heals your friendly target, or you if you have none, for 1,288 at level 50 before gear. Whoever it heals must be on one of your Strings. The others on your Strings get their 30% share (386).
- New ability: Tug (Skill Card level 12, instant, 6 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 40 m range). Heals an ally on one of your Strings for 276 at level 50 before gear, and its Strings heal twice as often for 4 sec. This multiplies with Haste.
- New ability: Cat's Cradle (Skill Card level 16, instant, 20 sec cooldown, 4 mana points). Heals every ally within 30 m, you included, for 460 at level 50 before gear and ties a loose String to each for 8 sec.
  - A loose String heals like one of your own and is none of your 6. It comes on top of the 3 an ally can hold.
  - An ally with a loose String counts as being on a String for shared heals, Hoist, Tug, Cupped Hands, Still Standing and Yank.
  - A loose String cannot be pulled taut, knotted, moved with Restring or cut with Cut the Strings.
- New ability: Wrench (Skill Card level 18, instant, 8 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 40 m range). Deals Shadow damage (460 at level 50 before gear) to an enemy that has one of your Strings. The ally it is crossed with is healed for the damage dealt, and that heal is shared along the Strings.
- New ability: Pull Taut (Skill Card level 20, instant, 15 sec cooldown, no cost, 40 m range). One String on your friendly target, or on you if you have none, delivers all the healing it had left at once: 32 for each second left at level 50 before gear, up to 483 from a fresh String. That String is used up and comes back after 10 sec.
- New ability: Cupped Hands (Skill Card level 22, instant, 25 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Puts a shield on an ally on one of your Strings for 5 sec. It absorbs 455 damage at level 50 before gear, and 202 more for each String on that ally: 1,063 with three.
- New ability: String Up (Skill Card level 24, instant, 30 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 30 m range). Hoists your enemy target off the ground for 3 sec. It cannot move, attack or cast. It needs no String and uses none.
- New ability: Restring (Skill Card level 26, instant, 20 sec cooldown, no cost, 40 m range, off the global cooldown). Moves the String you tied last to your current target: your friendly target, your enemy target, or you with no target.
  - The String counts as just tied: its time starts over, as does the time of the other Strings on that ally, and on an enemy it is crossed with the ally you tied last.
  - It takes no String from your hand.
  - It cannot move a String to an ally that already has 3 or an enemy that already has one, and it cannot move knotted Strings. With the newest String knotted, it takes the newest one that is not.
- New ability: Knot (Skill Card level 28, instant, 30 sec cooldown, no cost, 40 m range). The Strings on your friendly target, or on you if you have none, do not run out for 30 sec, and Restring cannot move them.
  - Only one ally can be knotted at a time. Knotting another unties the first, whose Strings then have 15 sec left at most.
  - When the 30 sec end the Strings run out, unless a String tied since then has given them more time.
- New ability: Tangle (Skill Card level 30, instant, 25 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 30 m range). Roots every enemy within 5 m of your target for 4 sec. It needs no String and uses none.
- New ability: Yank (Skill Card level 32, instant, 45 sec cooldown, no cost, 40 m range). Pulls your friendly target to 1.5 m from you. It must be on one of your Strings.
- New ability: Double Thread (Skill Card level 34, instant, 30 sec cooldown, no cost, off the global cooldown). Your next String on an ally within 10 sec is placed as two and takes only one from your hand.
  - The second String is none of your 6.
  - On an ally with room for only one more, one is placed and Double Thread is still used up. Restring does not use it.
- New ability: Behind Its Back (Skill Card level 36, instant, 60 sec cooldown, no cost, off the global cooldown). You take 40% less damage for 6 sec, damage over time included. You must have at least one String placed; loose Strings do not count.
- New ability: Slack (Skill Card level 38, instant, 60 sec cooldown, no cost). Your Strings stop healing for 4 sec, loose ones too, and you regain 1 mana point for each String you have out. Loose Strings give no mana. With 6 out that is 60% of your base mana.
- New ability: Still Standing (Skill Card level 42, instant, 120 sec cooldown, no cost, 40 m range). Goes on your friendly target, or on you if you have none; whoever it is must be on one of your Strings. For 8 sec, a killing blow leaves them at 1 health instead.
  - The Strings then hold it up for 4 sec: nothing can take its last health.
  - When the 4 sec end, it falls if it is below 25% health.
- New ability: Cut the Strings (Skill Card level 44, instant, 120 sec cooldown, no cost). Every String you have out snaps. Each one on an ally heals it for 552 at level 50 before gear (1,656 with three), and each one on an enemy deals 552 Shadow damage. All of them are back in your hand at once.
  - Strings that were used up before keep recovering at their own pace.
  - These heals are not shared along the Strings.
- New ability: Not Finished With You (Skill Card level 46, instant, 300 sec cooldown, no cost). Lifts the nearest downed ally within 40 m back onto their feet with 30% health and ties 3 Strings to them. It takes 3 Strings from your hand and cannot be used with fewer.

### New talents: the Strings
Ten talents for the healer. Six work with the Strings themselves and need no particular ability; four belong to one ability each and require it to be learned.
- New talent: First Stitch (2 ranks, row 3). Tying a String to an ally heals it at once, for as much as 2 / 4 heals of one String (64 / 129 at level 50 before gear).
  - It also heals when the String only starts the time of a full ally over, and when Restring moves a String to an ally. Double Thread's two Strings give one heal.
  - The heal has its own line on the healing meter: First Stitch. It is not shared along the Strings.
- New talent: Long Thread (2 ranks, row 3). Your Strings on allies last 2 / 4 sec longer: 17 / 19 sec. Strings on enemies still last 10 sec. String's tooltip shows the new time. Maxing it unlocks Spare Spool.
- New talent: Heave (2 ranks, row 3, requires Hoist). Hoist heals 5 / 10% more for each of your Strings on its target, up to 15 / 30% with three. Loose Strings do not count.
  - Capstone: on a target with 3 of your Strings, Hoist's cast time is 0.5 sec shorter.
- New talent: Spare Spool (2 ranks, row 4). Your Strings that were used up come back 1 / 2 sec sooner: after 9 / 8 sec.
- New talent: Full Harness (2 ranks, row 8). An ally that has 3 of your Strings takes 3 / 6% less damage of every kind. Loose Strings do not count.
- New talent: Taking the Weight (2 ranks, row 8). Crossed Strings moves 5 / 7% of the damage the ally takes to the enemy, instead of 3%.
  - Capstone: a crossing holds up to 40 m apart, instead of 30 m.
- New talent: Long Drop (2 ranks, row 8, requires String Up). When String Up ends, the enemy drops and takes Shadow damage (230 / 460 at level 50 before gear).
  - An enemy that dies while it hangs takes nothing. The damage has its own line on the damage meter: Long Drop.
- New talent: Snap Back (2 ranks, row 9, requires Pull Taut). Pull Taut has a 25 / 50% chance to put its String straight back in your hand instead of using it up. "Snap Back" appears over your character when it does.
- New talent: Give and Take (3 ranks, row 9, requires Slack). Slack stops your Strings healing for 1 / 2 / 3 sec less: 3 / 2 / 1 sec. The mana it gives is unchanged. Slack's tooltip shows the new time.
- New talent: Steady Hands (5 ranks, bottom row). Your Strings heal 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15% more. This applies to every String heal, loose Strings, Pull Taut and First Stitch. It does not apply to Cut the Strings.

### New talents: open to every build
Three more Shadow talents that work with every book.
- New talent: Many Hands (3 ranks, row 1). Your heals on allies other than yourself are 2 / 4 / 6% larger. This applies to every heal of yours on someone else. Shared heals and Wrench's heal copy another amount and are not increased again.
- New talent: Lean On Me (3 ranks, row 1). After you heal an ally other than yourself, you take 2 / 4 / 6% less damage for 4 sec, damage over time included. Every heal counts, including each heal of a String; another heal starts the 4 sec over.
- New talent: Left Hanging (2 ranks, row 2). Your stuns and roots last 10 / 20% longer. This applies to every stun and root you apply, from any book. The tooltips of those abilities show the new time.

### The Shadow talent tree
- The tree has 12 more slots: the edges of row 1, the middle of rows 2 and 3 with the two slots beside it in row 3, two inner slots each in rows 8 and 9, and two slots in the bottom row. Every row is still mirrored around the middle column.
- Spare Spool took the empty slot in row 4, and Long Drop the empty slot in row 8.
- No existing talent moved, so points already spent stay where they are.
- One slot is still empty: the bottom row, column 6.

### How it looks
- The demon hangs in the air behind you for as long as you have a String out: horned, red-hided, with long arms held out over you. It drifts after you and turns after you instead of being fixed to your back.
- A short thread runs from its hands towards each one it holds and fades out after 2.2 m. Nothing is strung across the screen.
- Threads hang over everyone on a String. They come down from above onto the head and shoulders and fade out 2.4 m up. There is one more thread for each String: two, three or four.
- The threads follow where their target stands but not how it turns, and they sway.
- Threads on an ally are pale. Threads on an enemy are red.
- String Up lifts the enemy 0.7 m off the ground on four threads. Tangle lays threads across the floor of its circle.
- Your party sees your demon, the threads, String Up and Tangle.
- Each of the nineteen abilities has an icon of its own.

### In a party
- Everything here works on party members and on you.
- The Strings, their heals, shared heals, Crossed Strings and Yank can be tried on the friendly training dummy. Still Standing only shows its buff there, because the dummy cannot die. Not Finished With You needs a downed party member.
- Strings never interact with another player's abilities, and two healers' Strings on the same ally are counted separately.

## Pacts
A new system for every character, whatever its build: a deal you take for a dungeon.

### How they work
- You hold one Pact at a time. Each has a bonus, a drawback and a vow: something that must never happen.
- Break the vow and the Pact turns against you until the dungeon ends: the bonus becomes the same amount as a penalty, and the drawback stays.
- Pacts are taken and given up in the main menu, on the new Pacts tab above Rune Spells in Abilities & Talents. They cannot be changed inside a dungeon or the Training Room.
- The Pact you hold is saved and stays with you from dungeon to dungeon.
- A broken Pact is whole again when you start a new dungeon. The next floor of the same dungeon does not restore it. In the Training Room it is whole again each time you enter.
- In the game your Pact shows as a buff that says what it does and what its vow is. A broken Pact shows as a debuff. Neither has a timer.
- When a Pact breaks, it is announced over your character and in the log with the reason and the penalty.
- What a Pact adds to or takes from your damage has its own line on the damage meter, under the Pact's name.
- Two Pacts are party only. Alone in a dungeon they do nothing: no bonus, no drawback and no vow.

### The ten Pacts
- New Pact: Pact of the Unbowed. Bonus: you deal 15% more damage. Drawback: you take 10% more damage. Vow: never go down or die. Broken: you deal 15% less damage and still take 10% more.
- New Pact: Pact of the Thin Line. Bonus: 12% more chance to critically strike. Drawback: 10% less maximum health. Vow: never drop below 25% health. Broken: 12% less chance to critically strike, and still 10% less maximum health.
- New Pact: Pact of the Relentless. Bonus: 10% Haste. Drawback: you move 15% slower. Vow: never go 5 sec in combat without hitting an enemy or healing an ally. Broken: 10% less Haste, and you still move 15% slower.
  - Every hit of yours on an enemy counts except the ticks of damage over time. Every heal of yours on an ally other than yourself counts.
  - Time spent stunned or running between enemies counts against you.
- New Pact: Pact of the Sidestep. Bonus: all your cooldowns recover 20% faster. Drawback: your abilities cost 10% more, in Mana, Rage or Energy. Vow: never take a single hit for more than 25% of your maximum health. Broken: your cooldowns recover 20% slower, and your abilities still cost 10% more.
  - Damage a shield absorbs does not count toward the hit.
  - The global cooldown is not affected.
- New Pact: Pact of the Purist. Bonus: you deal 20% more damage. Drawback: you take 5% more damage. Vow: never use an ability from any book but the one you chose. Broken: you deal 20% less damage and still take 5% more.
  - You choose its book when you take it, by pressing that book's icon on the Pact's card.
  - The basic abilities every character knows count: Heal belongs to Holy, Stoneguard and Taunt to Martial. Rune spells count as well.
  - Auto attacks and on-use items do not break it.
  - The first press of an ability that would break it only warns you. Press it again within 3 sec to use it anyway.
  - The Pact breaks when the ability goes off. A cast you cancel does not break it.
- New Pact: Pact of the Fast. Bonus: you take 10% less damage. Drawback: healing you receive is 15% smaller. Vow: never heal yourself with a spell or ability. Broken: you take 10% more damage, and healing you receive is 30% smaller.
  - Any heal of your own spell or ability that lands on you breaks it, including an area heal that includes you and a String on yourself. There is no warning.
  - Lifesteal, heals from talents and runes, natural regeneration and heals from allies do not break it.
  - The smaller healing applies to everything that heals you, Lifesteal included.
- New Pact: Pact of the Shepherd (party only). Bonus: your heals are 15% larger. Drawback: your heals on yourself are 30% smaller. Vow: never let a party member go down within 40 m of you. Broken: your heals are 15% smaller, and on yourself still 30% smaller.
- New Pact: Pact of the Vanguard (party only). Bonus: you take 12% less damage. Drawback: you deal 10% less damage. Vow: never let an enemy that is attacking you turn on a party member while you are alive. Broken: you take 12% more damage and still deal 10% less.
  - An enemy another player taunts off you breaks it. An enemy that goes for a decoy or resets does not.
- New Pact: Pact of the Restless. Bonus: Mana, Rage and Energy come in 25% faster. Drawback: out of combat you lose 1% of your maximum health every second, and you do not regenerate health at all. Vow: never stay out of combat for more than 30 sec once your first fight has started. Broken: Mana, Rage and Energy come in 25% slower, and you still lose health out of combat.
  - The health loss and the 30 sec only start with your first fight on a floor, and both stop once the dungeon is cleared.
  - The health loss never takes your last point of health.
  - Nothing is counted while you are dead or down.
- New Pact: Pact of the Reservoir. Bonus: your mana costs are 20% lower. Drawback: 15% less maximum mana. Vow: never drop below 15% mana. Broken: your mana costs are 20% higher, and you still have 15% less maximum mana.

### The main menu
- The Pacts tab sits above the Rune Spells tab. The two tabs together are exactly as tall as the Rune Spells tab was alone, so the books did not move.
- The tab shows the Pact you hold. Opening it lists all ten with their bonus, drawback, vow and what breaking them costs.
- The books keep one fixed order on every screen. The talent points text beside the row of book icons now has a fixed width, so the icons no longer shift when the numbers change.
