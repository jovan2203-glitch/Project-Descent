# Patch notes — 16 October 2026 (second update)

Heals and damage are given at level 50 before gear. Mana costs are in mana points; one point is 10% of your base mana. Bow abilities are given with a basic bow.

## Items

- New item: Worn Tome (Off Hand). Intellect and Spell Power.
- New item: Ashwood Wand (Off Hand). Spell Power and Critical Strike Rating.
- New item: Brass Lantern (Off Hand). Armor, Strength and Constitution. Made for the Holy tank.
- New item: Ritual Dagger (one-handed dagger, Main Hand or Off Hand). Intellect and Spell Power.
- New item: Runed Blade (one-handed sword, Main Hand or Off Hand). Intellect and Spell Power.
- All five can drop from enemies, elites and bosses. Off Hand items are not weapons: they do not attack. Some abilities now require an Off Hand item (tome, wand or lantern); shield abilities still require a shield.

## Holy

### Holy tank: the Censer (new playstyle: Censer Bearer)
Heavy armor, a melee weapon and an Off Hand item. Every ability below requires both.
- Your first censer ability makes a censer of light appear at your side, hanging on a chain of light. It fades 3 sec after combat ends. Your party sees it.
- Incense is the light in your censer: a buff of up to 100 stacks that lasts 30 sec, refreshed whenever you gain more. Each stack gives 0.15% Block (15% at 100), shown as a faint barrier of light that flashes when you block. Enemies within 5 m take up to 28 Holy damage every second at 100 stacks, with 4x threat. This has its own line on the damage meter: Incense.
- Each hit you take gives 1 Incense, each hit you block 2.
- Motes of light: your abilities knock motes loose from enemies, and spending Incense drops motes worth 60% of what you spent (at most 5 per spend). A mote lies on the ground for 10 sec. Walk within 1.2 m of one to pick it up: it gives 10 Incense and heals you for 101 (its own line on the healing meter: Light Mote). You can have 12 on the ground; the oldest fades first. Only you can pick up yours; your party sees them faint.
- New ability: Censer Swing (Skill Card level 2, instant, 0.4 mana points). Deals 322 Holy damage with 4x threat, gives 3 Incense and knocks a mote of light loose from the target.
- New ability: Hallowed Arc (Skill Card level 4, instant, 8 sec cooldown, 1 mana point). Deals 230 Holy damage with 4x threat to all enemies within 8 m, gives 1 Incense for each enemy hit and knocks a mote loose from each, up to 3.
- New ability: Guiding Ray (Skill Card level 6, instant, 10 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 25 m range). Deals 184 Holy damage with 6x threat, gives 3 Incense and drops a mote at the enemy.
- New ability: Absolve (Skill Card level 8, instant, 10 sec cooldown, 1.5 mana points). Spends up to 40 Incense and heals you for up to 2,024.
- New ability: Pour Out (Skill Card level 12, instant, 12 sec cooldown, 2 mana points). Needs at least 30 Incense and spends all of it: 13.8 Holy damage for each Incense spent (1,380 at 100) with 6x threat to all enemies within 8 m.
- New ability: Gather the Light (Skill Card level 16, instant, off the global cooldown, 30 sec cooldown). Every mote of light you have flies back to you and is picked up.
- New ability: Aureole (Skill Card level 20, instant, off the global cooldown, 20 sec cooldown). Spends 30 Incense: +25% Block for 5 sec.
- New ability: Raise the Censer (Skill Card level 28, instant, 90 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 30 m range). Your censer rises 4 m over an enemy for 8 sec. Enemies within 10 m of it must attack you for 4 sec, and it sheds a mote every second. While it is away, your Incense gives no Block and no aura; it comes back with it.
- New ability: Unfallen (Skill Card level 36, instant, off the global cooldown, 120 sec cooldown). For 8 sec nothing can kill you and hits give twice the Incense. When it ends, you heal for 30% of the damage you took during it.
- Mana: nonstop tanking runs out of mana after several minutes.

### Second Holy healer: the Prisms (new playstyle: Line of Light)
A healer who places floating prisms. Its spells need no weapon.
- Prisms: you place up to 3 white prisms; each lasts 1 min. Your direct heals send a beam of light from you to the ally, and every white prism the beam passes through (within 1 m of its line) stores 25% of the heal as light, up to 3,036, and makes the heal 10% stronger. A shattering prism heals the allies within 8 m with its light, split evenly between them, and deals up to 460 Holy damage to the enemies there (its own line on the meters: Prism Shatter). Your heals never target a prism.
- Click a prism to make it your prism target (a green ring). Abilities used on a prism use the one under your mouse (with Mouseover casting), then your prism target, then your closest.
- New ability: Set Prism (Skill Card level 2, instant, off the global cooldown, 8 sec cooldown, 0.5 mana points, 30 m range). Places a white prism.
- New ability: Gleam (Skill Card level 4, instant, 1 mana point, 40 m range). Heals an ally for 506.
- New ability: Bright Lance (Skill Card level 6, 2.5 sec cast, 3.5 mana points, 40 m range). Heals an ally for 1,822.
- New ability: Mending Ray (Skill Card level 8, 3 sec channel, 3 mana points, 40 m range). Heals an ally for 1,518 over the channel in 6 ticks; each tick's beam charges prisms.
- New ability: Shatter Prism (Skill Card level 10, instant). Shatters one of your white prisms.
- New ability: Move Prism (Skill Card level 14, instant, off the global cooldown, 5 sec cooldown, 1 mana point, 30 m range). Your prism closest to your mouse flickers over to the spot under your mouse, keeping its light and time.
- New ability: Prism Ward (Skill Card level 18, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 2 mana points, 40 m range). Shields an ally for 1,214 for 8 sec. Its beam charges prisms.
- New ability: Piercing Light (Skill Card level 22, 1.5 sec cast, 1.5 mana points, 30 m range). Deals 460 Holy damage to an enemy; white prisms on its beam store 25% of the damage as light.
- New ability: Realign (Skill Card level 26, instant, 20 sec cooldown, 30 m range). All your prisms flicker over to a spot in the same shape, keeping their light; their time starts over.
- New ability: Infuse (Skill Card level 30, instant, 45 sec cooldown, 1 mana point). Fills one of your white prisms to full.
- New ability: Shatter All (Skill Card level 36, instant, off the global cooldown, 20 sec cooldown). Shatters every white prism you have.
- New ability: Kaleidoscope (Skill Card level 44, instant, 2 min cooldown). For 10 sec your heals' beams pass through every white prism you have, wherever it stands.

### Holy archer: Lightstring (new playstyle)
A bow and Energy. It places gold prisms.
- Prism colours: white prisms (the healer's) work only with heals; gold prisms (the archer's) work only with arrows. Light never moves between them. Both colours count toward the limit of 3 prisms; a new one replaces your oldest of either colour.
- Refraction: each gold prism a shot passes through fires a refracted arrow at another enemy within 8 m of it, for 40% of the shot's damage (its own meter line: Refraction). With no other enemy there, the shot is 10% stronger instead. The prism also stores 25% of the shot's damage as light. Your auto-shots do this too, refracting for 20% and storing 10%.
- New ability: Glimmer Shot (Skill Card level 2, instant, 20 Energy). Deals 207 Holy damage.
- New ability: Prism Arrow (Skill Card level 4, instant, off the global cooldown, 15 Energy, 2 charges recharging every 12 sec, 30 m range). Plants a gold prism.
- New ability: Refracting Shot (Skill Card level 8, 2 sec cast, 8 sec cooldown, 30 Energy). Deals 414 Holy damage; its refracted arrows hit for 60% instead of 40%.
- New ability: Sunlit Arrow (Skill Card level 12, instant, 12 sec cooldown, 30 Energy). Deals 248 Holy damage and drains all the light from the gold prisms it passes through: 91 more damage for each full prism.
- New ability: Prism Breaker (Skill Card level 16, instant, 15 sec cooldown, 25 Energy). Shoots one of your gold prisms: it shatters for 276 Holy damage, up to 458 with full light, to all enemies within 8 m.
- New ability: Step of Light (Skill Card level 24, instant, off the global cooldown, 20 sec cooldown, 40 m range). You appear next to one of your prisms of either colour.
- New ability: Spectrum Volley (Skill Card level 36, instant, 2 min cooldown). For 8 sec, each gold prism shoots the nearest enemy within 8 m of it every second for 184 Holy damage, up to 245 with full light (its own meter line).

## Systems & Interface

- New: Mouseover casting (Settings, Interface tab, off by default). Pressing an ability while your mouse is over a legal target for it (an enemy, an ally or yourself, or one of your prisms) uses it on that target without changing your target. Your target ring and frame keep showing your real target.
