# Patch notes — 11 October 2026

## Shadow melee: the Hounds
A new way to play the Shadow book: a melee damage dealer with two demon dogs that fight beside it. It runs on Rage. All seven new abilities require a melee weapon of any kind.

### The Hounds
- While you have no Hounds, using a Shadow melee ability calls two of them up through the ground. They stay for 30 sec. The abilities that call them are Sic, Open Up, Both At Once, Run Them Down, Guard Dogs, Off the Chain and Leeching Strike.
- The Hounds are shown as a buff with the time they have left. Nothing renews them while they are out.
- They always bite the last enemy you damaged directly: every hit of a spell, an ability or an auto attack sends them to its enemy, whether or not it is your target. Damage over time and procs do not move them. When one spell or ability hits several enemies at the same moment, they go to your target if it was among them.
- Sent at an enemy that is more than one second's run away (9 m), the Hounds dash to it at more than four times their running speed, and their next bite waits only for the dash. They run to anything closer, and they never dash back to heel.
- Each Hound bites every 1.5 sec for Shadow damage (69 at level 50 before gear). Haste makes them bite faster.
- The Hounds have no stats of their own. Their hits use your Attack Power, Spell Power and critical strike chance, get every bonus to your Shadow damage, heal you through Lifesteal and make threat for you.
- Their hits count as procs. Effects that react to your own spell and ability hits do not react to them, they do not use up "your next spell or ability" buffs, and they add nothing to a Breach without the talent Out of the Hole. Effects that react to any Shadow damage, such as Dusk till Dawn, still count them.
- Enemies cannot target or damage the Hounds.
- If their prey dies or is more than 30 m from you, they come back to heel until you damage something.
- When their 30 sec are up, each Hound drags at its prey for Shadow damage (345 at level 50 before gear), and your next Shadow melee ability calls a new pair. This hit is called Drag Down. If you die or go down, they leave without it.
- Their damage has its own lines on the damage meter: Hound Bite, Hound Lunge and Drag Down.
- "Hounds" and "Roused" are keywords: they are shown in orange and explained when you hold Shift.

### Roused
- Any ability that costs Rage makes your Hounds Roused for 8 sec: their bites deal 25% more damage. This includes Rage abilities of other books.
- Spending Rage again starts the 8 sec over. It does not stack.
- What it adds has its own line on the damage meter: Roused.

### New abilities
- New ability: Sic (Skill Card level 6, no cooldown, generates 15 Rage). Strikes the target for 100% weapon damage as Shadow damage and sets your Hounds on it.
- New ability: Open Up (Skill Card level 10, 6 sec cooldown, generates 25 Rage). Strikes the target for 140% weapon damage as Shadow damage and leaves it Laid Open for 8 sec: the bites of your Hounds deal 30% more damage to it. The mark is your own; it does nothing for another player's Hounds. What it adds has its own line on the damage meter: Laid Open.
- New ability: Both At Once (Skill Card level 14, 4 sec cooldown, 40 Rage). Strikes the target for 120% weapon damage as Shadow damage, and each Hound lunges at it for Shadow damage (276 at level 50 before gear). Rouses your Hounds.
- New ability: Run Them Down (Skill Card level 18, 8 sec cooldown, 30 Rage). Strikes all enemies within 5 m for 60% weapon damage as Shadow damage. For 6 sec each bite of your Hounds also hits up to 2 other enemies within 5 m of the one bitten, for 50% of the bite's damage. Rouses your Hounds. Up to 5 enemies take the strike's full damage; with more, that damage is split evenly between all of them.
- New ability: Guard Dogs (Skill Card level 24, 30 sec cooldown, 20 Rage). For 5 sec you take 30% less damage. Your Hounds keep biting. Rouses your Hounds.
- New ability: Bring It Down (Skill Card level 28, 10 sec cooldown, no cost, generates no Rage). Sends your Hounds back under the ground at once. Each deals its Drag Down to its prey (345 Shadow damage at level 50 before gear), exactly as when their time is up. Your next Shadow melee ability calls a new pair.
  - It can only be used while your Hounds are out and have something to drag down. If they are at heel, they take your target, which must be alive and within 30 m.
  - It does not call Hounds and does not Rouse them. It is on the global cooldown.
  - The new pair starts with its full time. Roused ends when the Hounds leave. Off the Chain keeps running, but the time it added to the pair that left is lost.
- New ability: Off the Chain (Skill Card level 40, 90 sec cooldown, no cost, off the global cooldown). For 10 sec your Hounds bite 50% faster, and they stay 10 sec longer.

### Changed
- Leeching Strike is part of this kit: it calls the Hounds when none are out, and it Rouses them. Its damage, heal, cost and cooldown are unchanged.

### New talents: the Hounds
Thirteen talents for this build, on the two edges of the Shadow book. The seven on the left edge and in the bottom row work whichever ability called the Hounds. The six on the right edge each belong to one ability and require it to be learned.
- New talent: Lean and Hungry (3 ranks, row 4). Your Hounds bite 4 / 8 / 12% faster. This multiplies with Haste and Off the Chain. Maxing it unlocks Fed.
- New talent: Fed (2 ranks, row 5). A bite of your Hounds that critically strikes gives you 2 / 4 Rage. Only bites count, not lunges, Drag Down or the hits Run Them Down adds. Maxing it unlocks Worked Up.
- New talent: Worked Up (2 ranks, row 6). Roused lasts 2 / 4 sec longer.
  - Capstone: while your Hounds are Roused, their bites have 10% more chance to critically strike.
- New talent: Out of the Hole (2 ranks, row 7, requires Break Ground). The bites of your Hounds add 25 / 50% of the damage they deal to your Breach under the enemy while it is open.
- New talent: Long Night (2 ranks, row 8). Your Hounds stay 3 / 6 sec longer. Maxing it unlocks Take It With You.
- New talent: Take It With You (2 ranks, row 9). Drag Down deals 25 / 50% more damage. What it adds has its own line on the damage meter.
  - Capstone: if Drag Down kills the enemy, your next pair of Hounds arrives already Roused. The kill counts when the enemy dies by your hand within 1.5 sec of Drag Down hitting it.
- New talent: Pack Leader (5 ranks, bottom row). Your Hounds deal 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15% more damage. It applies to bites, lunges and Drag Down, and has its own line on the damage meter.
- New talent: Still Wet (2 ranks, row 3, requires Open Up). Laid Open lasts 2 / 4 sec longer. Open Up's tooltip shows the new time.
  - Capstone: your own direct hits also deal 10% more damage to that enemy. Damage over time and procs do not get it. What it adds has its own line on the damage meter.
- New talent: Share the Kill (2 ranks, row 4, requires Leeching Strike). Leeching Strike's heal is 50 / 100% larger while your Hounds are out. A Leeching Strike that calls the Hounds already gets it.
- New talent: Pincer (3 ranks, row 5, requires Both At Once). The lunges of Both At Once deal 10 / 20 / 30% more damage. What it adds has its own line on the damage meter. Maxing it unlocks Again.
  - Capstone: a lunge that critically strikes gives you 10 Rage back.
- New talent: Again (2 ranks, row 6, requires Both At Once). Both At Once has a 15 / 30% chance to reset its own cooldown. The word "Again" appears over your character when it does.
- New talent: Stand Over (2 ranks, row 7, requires Guard Dogs). During Guard Dogs, each bite of your Hounds heals you for 1 / 2% of your maximum health. The heal has its own line on the healing meter.
- New talent: Slipped Leash (2 ranks, row 9, requires Off the Chain). Off the Chain lasts 2 / 4 sec longer, and your Hounds stay that much longer as well. Off the Chain's tooltip shows the new time.
  - Capstone: a third Hound joins your pair for as long as Off the Chain lasts. It bites like the other two, lunges with Both At Once, and deals its own Drag Down when it leaves or when Bring It Down is used. A pair called while Off the Chain is still running gets the third Hound too.

### New talents: open to every build
Two more Shadow talents that work with every book, on the edges of row 2.
- New talent: Thick Blood (3 ranks). Your Rage decays 15 / 30 / 45% slower.
- New talent: Feeding Frenzy (3 ranks). When an enemy you damaged dies, you gain 2 / 4 / 6% Haste for 8 sec. Another death starts the 8 sec over; it does not stack.

### The Shadow talent tree
- The tree has 9 more slots: rows 2, 4, 5 and 7 gained their two edge slots, and the bottom row gained a middle slot. Every row is still mirrored around the middle column.
- The other six new talents took slots that were empty.
- No existing talent moved, so points already spent stay where they are.
- Two slots are still empty: row 4, column 5 and row 8, column 6.

### How they look
- The Hounds are in the game world: large demon dogs with a red hide, violet horns and spines, a barbed tail and burning eyes.
- They climb out of a hole in the ground next to your target, or beside you if you have none.
- They stand on either side of their prey, away from you, snap at it when a bite lands and leap at it for Both At Once. With nothing to bite they follow at your heel.
- In a dash they run low and stretched out with their jaws open, and leave a red streak behind them.
- The third Hound of Slipped Leash walks behind the pair at heel and takes the far side of their prey.
- When their time is up they sink back into the ground.
- Your party sees your Hounds too, including the dash and the third Hound.

## Runes
- New Legendary rune: Dusk till Dawn. Dealing Shadow damage gives you Dusk: you deal 20% more Shadow damage for 12 sec. When Dusk ends you gain Dawn: you deal 20% more Holy damage for 12 sec.
  - Any Shadow damage starts Dusk, including damage over time and procs. The hit that starts it is not increased.
  - Dusk cannot be refreshed, and a new Dusk cannot start until Dawn has ended. The two are never active at the same time.
  - Both bonuses apply to spells, abilities and damage over time.
  - Dawn only follows a Dusk that ran its full time. If Dusk is lost early, for example by dying, there is no Dawn.
  - Only one Legendary rune can be equipped at a time.
- Mysterious Runes from chests can be Legendary again, now that a Legendary rune exists.

## Arcane melee
- Changed: Encore no longer has a cooldown (it was 10 sec). It still needs at least one Trace to replay, and it is on the global cooldown.
- Three talent effects only shortened or reset Encore's cooldown. They have new effects. Points already spent in them stay.
- Changed: Curtain Call (2 ranks, requires Encore). When Encore replays 3 or more Traces, each replay deals 10 / 20% more damage. It was 1 / 2 sec off Encore's cooldown. What it adds has its own line on the damage meter.
- Changed: Full House (2 ranks, requires Wide Cut). When Wide Cut hits 3 or more enemies, you gain Full House for 6 sec: your next Encore also replays each Trace on up to 1 / 2 other enemies within 5 m of its target, for 50% of its damage. It was 1 / 2 sec off Encore's cooldown.
  - The Encore uses Full House up, even if no other enemy is in reach. Another Wide Cut into 3 or more enemies starts its 6 sec over.
  - The other enemies are chosen when Encore is used. Their hits cannot critically strike or be avoided, leave no Traces, and get Curtain Call's bonus.
  - This damage has its own line on the damage meter: Full House.
- Changed: Dress Rehearsal's capstone. The first Encore during Double Time does not use up your Traces. It was: using Double Time resets Encore's cooldown. Double Time still lasts 2 / 4 sec longer.
  - A buff named Dress Rehearsal shows that the free Encore is still available. It ends when Double Time ends.
- Changed: when Double Time ends while you hold more Traces than your usual limit (3, or 4 with Standing Ovation's capstone), you keep all of them. Until now your next strike pushed out every Trace above the limit.
  - While you hold more than the limit, your strikes leave no new Traces and push none out. The Traces you hold can still be replayed with Encore, and they still run out one by one.
  - Once you are back at the limit or below it, strikes leave Traces as usual.
  - Double Time's tooltip says so.
