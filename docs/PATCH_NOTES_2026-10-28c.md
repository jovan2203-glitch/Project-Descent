# Patch notes — 28 October 2026 (3)

Tanks no longer depend on what sits on their action bars: a tank's own abilities deal less damage for more threat, and nothing else changes for anyone. Auto attacks now do much less of a weapon user's damage, and weapon abilities hit harder to make up for it. Also: a Martial interrupt, a new Holy crowd control, and buffs for buttons that were never worth pressing.

## Fire

- Changed: Searing Strike, Quench, Flare, Fold Steel and Red Edge deal 28% more damage (Searing Strike 192% weapon damage, was 150%; Quench and Flare 128%, were 100%; Fold Steel 64% per hit, was 50%; Red Edge 32% per auto attack, was 25%).
- Changed: Flaming Arrow, Coal Shot, Pitch Arrow and Cinder Rain deal 30% more damage (Flaming Arrow 234% weapon damage, was 180%; Coal Shot 169%, was 130%; Pitch Arrow 104%, was 80%; Cinder Rain 33% per tick, was 25%).
- Changed: Brand deals 15% less damage.

## Frost

- Changed: Frost Strike, Hoarcleave, Calving, Icebreaker and Bitter Edge deal 34% more damage (Frost Strike 241% weapon damage, was 180%; Icebreaker 268%, was 200%, and 335% on a target you have Frozen, was 250%).
- Changed: Frost Arrow, Shatter Shot and Glacial Draw deal 35% more damage (Frost Arrow 176% weapon damage, was 130%; Shatter Shot 378%, was 280%; Glacial Draw 297%, was 220%).

## Lightning

- Changed: Charged Edge, Whiplash, Arc Cleave and Cadence deal 33% more damage (Charged Edge 359% weapon damage, was 270%).
- Changed: Stormbreak and Return Stroke deal 12% less damage (88% weapon damage, was 100%) and make 5x threat (was 4x).

## Arcane

- Changed: Glint deals 57% more damage.
- Changed: Collapse deals 29% more damage.
- Changed: Arcane Strike, Swift Cut, Deep Cut, Late Cut and Wide Cut deal 5% more damage.

## Shadow

- Changed: Sic, Leeching Strike, Open Up, Both At Once and Run Them Down deal 12% more damage (Sic and Open Up 156% weapon damage, were 140%).

## Nature

- Changed: Adder Venom, Asp Venom and Krait Venom deal 12% more damage.

## Holy

- New ability: Holy Ground (2 mana, 45 sec cooldown, instant, card level 26). Placed up to 30 m away: for 6 sec, no enemy can step within 5 m of the spot. Enemies already inside are pushed out to its edge. Bosses are too strong to be kept out.
- Changed: Censer Swing deals 10% less damage.
- Changed: Glimmer Shot and Sunlit Arrow deal 39% more damage (Glimmer Shot 250% weapon damage, was 180%; Sunlit Arrow 167%, was 120%).
- Changed: Refracting Shot deals 360% weapon damage (was 200%).

## Martial

- New ability: Stone Throw (no cost, 15 sec cooldown shared with every interrupt, instant, off the global cooldown, card level 16). Throw a stone at a target up to 25 m away. If it is casting, the cast is interrupted and it can't cast again for 3 sec. If it isn't, the stone knocks aside the melee hit it is winding up, and that hit never lands. Needs a melee weapon.
- Changed: The Wheel's fillers and heavy fillers (Hack, Chop, Slash, Lunge, Jab, Hook, Thud, Crush) deal 24% more damage.
- Changed: Cut, Whirl, Dart In, Kick Off and Twin Stab deal 12% more damage.
- Changed: Quick Shot, Pierce, Spread, Heavy Draw, Follow Up and Loose deal 25% more damage.
- Changed: Punch, Kick and Elbow deal 10% less damage.

## Systems & Interface

- Changed: The tank rule no longer looks at your action bars. Every hit from a tank spec's own kit (its abilities and everything they set off) deals 35% of its damage and makes 1.4x threat. Every other hit, including your auto attacks, deals its full damage, whatever else you have on your bars. Tooltips show the real numbers.
- Changed: Auto attacks and auto-shots deal 20% of your weapon's damage (was 50%).
- Changed: The role icon on party frames still comes from your action bars, but it is only a label now.
